# Rotating convertible car seats — comparison & recommendations

Eight seats, researched **2026-09-24**. Prices are Target sale prices as of that date.

Seven of the eight are rotating convertibles; the [Graco 4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md) does not rotate and is included as the value benchmark.

**Read first:** [Sources & methodology](sources.md) — explains why "look up the safety rating" has no direct answer here, and how the 1–5 scores were built.

---

## ⚠️ Before anything else

**NHTSA's official Ease-of-Use rating program covers 126 seats. None of these eight are in it.** Rotating convertibles are a new category and the rating program lags. Anyone quoting you a "NHTSA star rating" for these seats is quoting something else.

**Both Evenflo seats on this list are covered by an open federal compliance investigation.** NHTSA investigation **AQ24001**, opened January 5, 2024 and still open, states the Evenflo Revolve360 "appears not to comply with a number of FMVSS No. 213 requirements, including but not limited to the means of installation in S5.3.2 and labeling requirements in S5.5," and that NHTSA is "aware of at least six crash incidents in the field during which the Revolve360's shell... reportedly separated from its detachable base." The investigation explicitly extends to other Revolve360 products using detachable bases. NHTSA notes "Evenflo has continued to certify and offer the Revolve360 products for sale."

The [Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) is additionally under recall **25C010000** — 324,997 units, headrest foam choking hazard. Sibling models carry three further FMVSS 213 recalls.

If you already own either Evenflo seat: contact Evenflo at **1-800-233-5921**, check your manufacture date against nhtsa.gov/recalls, and make sure the seat is registered so you receive notice when a remedy is ordered.

## Recommendations

### 🥇 Buy: [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) — $279.99

Cheapest seat here, cleanest record of any rotating seat (1 complaint, 0 recalls, 0 investigations), rotates **both** directions, 17" wide, non-magnetic chest clip, and the best installation subscore of any rotating seat (4.17). 85% would recommend, flagged "rarely returned," and no subscore below 4.0 — the only rotating seat that can say that.

Trade-offs: newest model here, so the clean record rests on thin field history. No booster mode — you will buy another seat around 65 lb. Not FAA certified.

### 🥈 Runner-up: [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) — $359.99

Best *evidence* of any rotating seat. Zero complaints, zero recalls, zero investigations — and zero brand-wide across Joie's entire US catalogue. Its sibling **Joie Chili Spin 360 scored 5/5 rear-facing overall from NHTSA**, matched only by the $500–600 Cybex Sirona S and Nuna Revv. Three modes, 360° in every harness mode plus 180° in booster.

Trade-offs: priciest here. The **stiff, non-adjustable thigh bolsters** are the dominant complaint and must stay installed until 40 lb — comfort subscore 3.63. Sit your child in it before committing. Recline locks at install. No independent CSFTL teardown exists.

### 💰 Best value, if rotation is optional: [Graco 4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md) — $295.99

The best-evidenced purchase on the list, and it doesn't rotate. **The only seat whose platform carries a real NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating — 4/5 rear-facing, 5/5 forward-facing, 4/5 booster, with 5/5 instructions in all three modes.** The only unqualified CSFTL recommendation. 100% of owners would recommend it. Four modes to 120 lb, ten-year life, lightest seat here at 22.75 lb, best cover-cleaning system, and **$29.60/yr — cheapest cost of ownership in the group.**

Be honest about why you want rotation. If it's because bending into the car hurts — C-section recovery, bad back, tall vehicle — buy a rotating seat; this one won't help. If rotation just looked convenient, buy this and save money.

Trade-offs: **21.5" wide, the widest here** ("Slim" means 10% narrower at the cupholders than the standard 4Ever DLX, not narrow overall) — rules out three-across. Rear-facing tops out at 40 lb versus 50 lb on Britax and Evenflo.

### ⚠️ Conditional: [Britax Galaxy 360 Slim](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) — $343.99

Best hardware, worst ownership experience. ClickTight install, the **only integrated anti-rebound panel** here, one-piece seat/base (so shell separation is structurally impossible), **explicit FMVSS 213a certification**, rear-facing to 50 lb, 17" and fits three-across, zero NHTSA complaints.

Then: 4.11★, **29% would recommend**, **value subscore 2.73** — the lowest number anywhere in this dataset — plus a persistent clicking noise while driving, a rotation release that sticks on some units, and recline described as "extremely hard to navigate."

Buy only if ClickTight, the anti-rebound panel, or three-across fitment are specifically what you need — and buy where you can return it.

### ⏸️ Hold off: [Chicco Fit360 ClearTex](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) — $351.99

Nicest seat here to live with (comfort 4.64, installation 4.62, genuine LeverLock lock-off, second base available) — undermined by one component. **26 of its 35 NHTSA complaints are about the magnetic chest clip**, including one that "fell apart into multiple little pieces" mid-drive. Complaints rising: 12 in 2025 → 23 in the first nine months of 2026. No recall issued. Target reviews show the identical pattern independently.

Also: CSFTL declines to recommend it (a barefoot 3-year-old partially opened the lock-off panel with her feet), and a 6-year age cap on an 8-year seat gives it the **worst cost of ownership here at $58.66/yr**.

Revisit if Chicco redesigns the clip.

### 🚫 Pass: [Graco Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) — $319.99

Zero NHTSA complaints across 1,695 reviews is the strongest per-exposure record here. But **CSFTL pulled it from their recommended list in 2024** — the SnugLock lock-off proved unreliable and installs repeatedly loosened. An install that loosens is the failure mode that actually matters.

It also **rotates rear-facing only**, has a cramped rear-facing belt path CSFTL says to avoid if you have large hands or arthritis, and scores 3.81 on installation and 64% would recommend — on a seat $40 more than [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md).

Reconsider only if you *want* rear-facing-only rotation: the permanently attached forward-facing tether is a real safety plus.

### 🚫 Do not buy: [Evenflo Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md) — $349.99 and [Evenflo Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) — $291.99

Open federal compliance investigation, shell-base separation in six reported crashes, a 324,997-unit recall on the Slim, and three FMVSS 213 recalls across the Revolve platform in two years — including the Reo (headrest shifts in a crash) whose remedy was still "under development" as of July 2026.

The Extend has the **lowest recommend rate in the comparison at 55%**. The Slim has the **worst installation subscore at 3.48**. Owners of the *un-recalled* Extend report the same foam shedding that got the Slim recalled. Both have 90-day warranties; everything else here gives a year.

The 50 lb rear-facing limit and the Slim's 16.75" width are genuinely good. Not good enough. If you need 50 lb rear-facing, [Britax Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) delivers it with a clean record.

## If you buy nothing else from these notes

1. **Check the manufacture date sticker.** FMVSS 213a (side impact) applies by build date — after June 30, 2025. Recall ranges are defined by build date too. The marketing copy tells you nothing.
2. **Register the seat.** Recall notification depends on it. Two of the recalls documented here were themselves for missing registration cards.
3. **Get the install checked by a CPST — it's free.** Most seats that fail in the field fail on installation, not design. That single step outweighs every spec above.
4. **Rear-face as long as the seat allows.** It is the highest-leverage safety decision available to you, and it is free.
5. **Consumer Reports is worth a month's subscription** before a $300 purchase — they run a more severe crash protocol than FMVSS 213 and rate seats independently. Paywalled, so not included here.
