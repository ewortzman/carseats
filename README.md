# Convertible car seats on Target — full comparison & recommendations

**51 convertible car seats** available on Target.com, researched **2026-09-24**. Prices are Target sale prices as of that date.

"Convertible" here means the seat converts rear-facing → forward-facing. That includes 2-in-1 / 3-in-1 / 4-in-1 / all-in-one seats with booster modes, and rotating and non-rotating designs. It excludes infant carriers, harness-to-booster combination seats (forward-facing only) and belt-positioning boosters.

**Read this first:** [Sources & methodology](sources.md) — why "look up the NHTSA safety rating" has no direct answer for most of these seats, and how the 1–5 scores were built.

## ⚠️ Read before you buy anything on this list

**1. NHTSA's official Ease-of-Use rating program covers only 126 seats.** Of the 51 seats here, **14 have a real federal rating** (listed below). The rest do not. Anyone quoting you a "NHTSA star rating" for an unrated seat is quoting something else.

**2. Both Evenflo Revolve360 seats and the Gold variant are under an open federal compliance investigation.** NHTSA investigation **AQ24001**, opened January 5, 2024 and still open: the Revolve360 "appears not to comply with a number of FMVSS No. 213 requirements, including but not limited to the means of installation in S5.3.2 and labeling requirements in S5.5," and NHTSA is "aware of at least six crash incidents in the field during which the Revolve360's shell... reportedly separated from its detachable base." The [Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) is additionally under recall **25C010000** (324,997 units, headrest foam choking hazard). Evenflo: **1-800-233-5921**.

**3. The Maxi-Cosi Pria platform carries an unremedied recall.** **21C003000** — Pria 85, **119,863 units**, forward-facing lap-belt install without the top tether "may not adequately protect the child from head injury," FMVSS 213 non-compliance. NHTSA's record states **"No remedy has been established at this time."** Current [Pria Pure Cosi](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md) and [Pria Max](maxicosi-pria-max.md) stock is not the recalled seat, but **always use the top tether forward-facing on any Pria.** Dorel: **1-877-657-9546**.

**4. Britax One4Life Slim was recalled for a misstated lower-anchor limit.** **24C001000** — models E1D197A/B/F/G, labels and user guide state the wrong maximum rear-facing lower-anchor weight. Britax: **888-427-4829**. See [One4Life Slim](britax-one4life-slim.md).

**5. Three unrecalled defect patterns worth knowing:**
- [Chicco Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) / [Fit360 Zip](chicco-fit360-zip.md) — **26 of 35** federal complaints concern the magnetic chest clip detaching, one mid-drive. Trend rising. No recall.
- [Britax Poplar](britax-poplar.md) — **three of four** federal complaints report the **recline indicator printed backwards**, an airway risk for a newborn. No recall.
- [Evenflo Symphony](evenflo-symphony-extend-breeze.md) — **81 of 102** federal complaints involve the harness or buckle, dominated by **webbing fraying**; two filed by a certified CPST. No current recall.

**6. Check the manufacture date sticker on whatever you buy.** **FMVSS 213a** (side impact) is mandatory only for seats built after **June 30, 2025**. Only 9 of these 51 seats cite 213a on their listing at all.

## Recommendations

### 🥇 Best overall — [Graco 4Ever DLX 4-in-1](graco-4ever-dlx.md) · $255.99
The only seat here with a real NHTSA rating on its own platform at **4/5 rear-facing, 5/5 forward-facing, 4/5 booster — with 5/5 instructions in all three modes.** Cites **FMVSS 213a**. Integrated belt lock-off. Four modes, 4–120 lb, ten years, 23 lb. **6,173 ratings at 4.78★ with 89% would recommend** — the largest credible evidence base on this list. CSFTL recommends it. $29.60/yr, the cheapest cost of ownership here.
*Rule out if:* you need rotation, or three-across (21.5" wide), or to rear-face past 40 lb.

### 💰 Best value — [Chicco Fit3x ClearTex Ultra Slim](chicco-fit3x.md) · $183.99
**16.7" wide, 21 lb, three modes to 100 lb, steel LockSure belt lock-offs in both directions, explicit FMVSS 213a, GREENGUARD Gold flame-retardant-free fabric, zero complaints, 87% would recommend, no subscore below 4.4.** Nothing under $250 comes close. Owners confirm genuine three-across fitment.
*Caveat:* several owners report the rear-facing install is hard to get tight — have a CPST check it.

### 🔄 Best rotating — [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) · $279.99
Cheapest rotating seat here, rotates **both** directions, 17" wide, non-magnetic chest clip, 1 complaint / 0 recalls / 0 investigations, **85% would recommend**, install subscore 4.17 — best of any rotating seat. Flagged "rarely returned."
*Caveat:* newest model here so the clean record is thinly evidenced; no booster mode; not FAA certified.

### 🔄 Best premium rotating — [Joie Pepper Spin SI 4-in-1](joie-pepper-spin-si.md) · $449.99
**Four modes 4–120 lb, 360° in both harness modes plus 180° in booster, a two-state TurnLock green/red rotation indicator, LockTight lock-off usable with LATCH or belt, FMVSS 213a *and* 213b, 15-position harness, zero complaints on the cleanest brand record here.** $80 less than the [Evenflo Gold](evenflo-gold-revolve360-extend.md) and not under federal investigation.
*Caveat:* 32.4 lb — heaviest seat here; only 49 ratings.

### 📏 Best three-across — [Graco SlimFit3 LX](graco-slimfit3-lx.md) · $224.99 or [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) · $183.99
Both **16.7"**. The Graco is lighter (19.2 lb) with a bigger review base (1,363 at 4.74★) and install subscore 4.63; the Chicco adds a rear-facing lock-off, an explicit 213a claim and flame-retardant-free fabric for $41 less. Graco's weakness: repeated **head-slumping-forward** complaints.

### ⭐ Best measured rotating seat — [Joie Chili Spin SI](joie-chili-spin-si.md) · $339.99
Its rated sibling scored **5/5 rear-facing overall with 5/5 on vehicle-installation features and instructions** — the best rotating-seat score in NHTSA's entire dataset, matched only by the $500–600 Cybex Sirona S and Nuna Revv. But **4.08★** from owners, almost certainly the rigid non-adjustable bolsters. For $20 more the [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) adds a third mode on the same platform.

### 🛠 Best install hardware — [UPPAbaby Rove](uppababy-rove.md) · $499.99
Spring-loaded lock-off doors plus a **SmartSecure red-to-green belt-tension indicator**, rear-facing to 50 lb, always-active side-impact pods, GREENGUARD Gold, **3-year warranty**, **zero one- and two-star reviews across 105 ratings, 100% would recommend**.
*Rule out if:* you need a newborn seat — **14 lb minimum** — or one seat to cover the booster years.

### 🏷 Tightest budget — [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) · $127.99
Zero complaints across 2,930 ratings. But NHTSA rates it **2/5 rear-facing overall with 1/5 labels and 1/5 vehicle-installation features** — the weakest profile here. It is a safe seat that is easy to install wrong. For $22 more, [Graco TriRide](graco-triride-3in1.md) is rated **4/5 in all three modes with 5/5 instructions** (though its harness is hard to tighten). Either way, **book a free CPST inspection.**

## If you take nothing else from these notes

1. **Check the manufacture date sticker.** FMVSS 213a applies by build date (after June 30, 2025). Recall ranges are defined by build date too. Marketing copy tells you nothing.
2. **Register the seat.** Recall notification depends on it — and two of the recalls documented here were themselves for missing registration cards.
3. **Get the install checked by a CPST — it's free.** NHTSA scored **vehicle-installation features 1/5 or 2/5 on seven of the fourteen rated seats here.** That single step outweighs every spec in these notes.
4. **Rear-face as long as the seat allows.** Highest-leverage safety decision available to you, and free.
5. **Always use the top tether forward-facing** — especially on any Maxi-Cosi Pria, where a lap-belt-only forward-facing install is the exact condition of an unremedied 119,863-unit head-injury recall.
6. **Consumer Reports is worth a month's subscription** before a $300 purchase — they run a more severe crash protocol than FMVSS 213. Paywalled, so not included here.
