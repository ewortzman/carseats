## Safety

Same platform as the [Poplar](britax-poplar.md). **FMVSS 213 cited; no explicit FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing — check the manufacture date sticker.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for this model. The **Britax Poplar** is rated (mfg. June 2024) and is the nearest proxy:

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **4/5** | 4 | **5** | 3 | 4 |
| Forward-facing | **3/5** | 4 | **5** | **2/5** | **2/5** |

Britax's brand pattern: strong labels, weak install features and forward-facing instructions.

Rear-facing to 50 lb / 49" is among the best in this comparison.

Read the [Poplar page](britax-poplar.md) before buying: three of its four federal complaints report a **reversed recline indicator**, a newborn airway risk on this shared platform. The Poplar S record is clean, but with 107 ratings that may reflect exposure rather than a corrected part. Verify the recline with a CPST either way.

## Reliability & owner feedback

4.67★ from 107 ratings. Target does not publish attribute subscores on this listing.

Best-rated Britax in this comparison alongside the base [Poplar](britax-poplar.md), and far ahead of [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) (4.11★, 29% recommend) and [One4Life](britax-one4life.md) (4.03★, 28% recommend).

Expect the Poplar ownership profile: excellent ClickTight install, genuinely slim, painful cover washing with non-removable strap pads, fiddly squeeze-style LATCH connectors.

**CSFTL reviewed the [base Poplar](britax-poplar.md) favourably** — "a solid addition to Britax's lineup" that "really shines in rear facing mode." Carry over one warning: **vehicle headrests fouled the forward-facing install in every car CSFTL tested, so they do not recommend the Poplar platform for vehicles with non-removable headrests.** Their measurements: 17" at the headrest, 20" with cupholders installed, 26 lb.

## Pros

- ClickTight install

## Cons

- **$12 more than the [Poplar](britax-poplar.md) for trim only** — no functional upgrade documented
- Platform sibling has three federal complaints about a **backwards recline indicator**; verify yours
- **No FMVSS 213a claim**
- **No anti-rebound bar** — sold separately
- NHTSA forward-facing: 2/5 install features, 2/5 instructions
- **2 modes only** — no booster
- Cover washing painful; strap pads not removable
- No published expiration

## Conclusion

Functionally the same seat as the [Poplar](britax-poplar.md) with nicer fabric for $12. Pick on colour; there is no safety or capability reason to choose between them.

Both are the sensible Britax choice over [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) (rotates, 29% would recommend) and [One4Life](britax-one4life.md) (four modes, 28% would recommend, value 2.5).

Have the recline angle checked by a CPST regardless of which Poplar you buy.
