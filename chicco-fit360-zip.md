## Safety

**Cites FMVSS 213a explicitly** — Advanced Side Impact Protection "engineered to meet the Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard FMVSS 213a – Side Impact." GREENGUARD Gold Certified, FAA approved.

**Notable: this listing documents an anti-rebound bar on the Fit360 base** — a feature the standard [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) listing does not mention. Same base, so the standard seat almost certainly has it too. That puts the Fit360 platform alongside [Britax Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) as one of only two rotating seats here with an ARB.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating.** Chicco's rated seats: **OneFit ClearTex** 3/3/3, **Fit4** 4/4/3 — see [OneFit LX](chicco-onefit-lx.md).

**The magnetic chest clip is unchanged from the standard Fit360**, where it accounts for **26 of 35** NHTSA complaints, with reports of the clip detaching under light pressure and one coming apart mid-drive. The Zip's zipper solves a laundry problem, not that one.

## Reliability & owner feedback

4.83★ from 79 ratings — **the highest star average of any seat in this comparison**; 92% five-star, 3% one-star. "100% would recommend" is based on a single recommendation, and all-5.0 subscores come from one detailed review — treat both as noise.

Praise: rock-solid install — "There is literally ZERO move in the base"; one owner installed it in 15 minutes after giving up on a rival seat. One-hand rotation — "No more back pain or frustration." Magnetic clip and strap magnets ease loading a squirmy toddler. Zip-off cover washing and reinstalling reported as easy. The included base mirror beats headrest mirrors that keep falling off.

Complaints: bulky front-to-back — one owner could not slide the front seats back in a 2023 Camry; weak side magnets that "don't stay in place when getting baby out"; heavy to move between bases; one parent bought a cover to stop their child kicking the base release (echoing CSFTL's finding on the standard Fit360 that a barefoot 3-year-old could partly open the lock-off panel).

## Conclusion

The Zip fixes the standard [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md)'s cover problem and throws in the $39.99 mirror, and owners love it — 4.83★ is the best average on this list. It also confirms the base has an anti-rebound bar, which is a real safety feature.

It does not fix the reason to avoid the Fit360. The magnetic chest clip is identical, and 26 owners have told NHTSA it comes apart — one of them mid-drive without noticing. Paying $89 more for a zipper does not change that, and at $440.99 across a 6-year usable window this is the most expensive seat per year of use in the comparison.

If you want a rotating Chicco, wait for a clip redesign. If you want a rotating seat now, [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) is $161 less with a clean record, or [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) $81 less with three modes and the best NHTSA-rated sibling here.
