## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for the Ellaris in either branding. Rated Dorel siblings all score **2/5 rear-facing overall** with **1/5 rear-facing vehicle-installation features** — [Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md), [EverSlim](safety1st-everslim.md), [Trimate](safety1st-trimate.md), [Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md). There is no reason to expect the entry-level Ellaris to beat the range above it.

Disney Baby car seats are **rebadged Dorel Juvenile Group products** — the same shells, harnesses and hardware as the [Safety 1st](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) equivalents with licensed Disney fabric. They appear in NHTSA's database under make "DISNEY BABY" with the Safety 1st model names.

Every rated Dorel all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the Grow and Go, SlimRide EverSlim, Trimate and Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One.

Disney versions typically carry a **price premium of $20–70 over the Safety 1st original** for the fabric. You are paying for the licence, not for engineering.

Skip. The 5.0★ is ten reviews on a seat whose identical Safety 1st twin rates **3.48★ across 101 reviews** — trust the larger sample.

At $129.99 you are $2 away from [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) ($127.99), which adds booster mode to 100 lb and has 2,930 ratings. $20 further gets [Graco TriRide](graco-triride-3in1.md) with a verified **4/5 in all three modes**.

There is no scenario in which this is the right seat to buy.
