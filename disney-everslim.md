## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing.

**Official NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** — for the **Safety 1st SlimRide EverSlim** (mfg. March 2024), the identical platform:

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **2/5** | 3 | **2/5** | **1/5** | 3 |
| Forward-facing | **3/5** | 3 | **2/5** | 3 | 4 |
| Booster | **3/5** | **1/5** | 3 | **5** | 5 |

**2/5 rear-facing overall, 1/5 rear-facing vehicle-installation features, 1/5 for securing the child in booster.** Booster install features score 5/5 — but in booster mode the seat is essentially a belt guide.

Disney Baby car seats are **rebadged Dorel Juvenile Group products** — the same shells, harnesses and hardware as the [Safety 1st](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) equivalents with licensed Disney fabric. They appear in NHTSA's database under make "DISNEY BABY" with the Safety 1st model names.

Every rated Dorel all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the Grow and Go, SlimRide EverSlim, Trimate and Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One.

Disney versions typically carry a **price premium of $20–70 over the Safety 1st original** for the fabric. You are paying for the licence, not for engineering.
