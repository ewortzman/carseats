## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing.

**Official NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** — the rating is for the **Safety 1st Grow and Go** (mfg. May 2022), the identical platform:

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **2/5** | **2/5** | **1/5** | **1/5** | 3 |
| Forward-facing | **3/5** | 5 | **1/5** | 3 | 4 |
| Booster | **3/5** | **2/5** | 3 | 3 | 5 |

**2/5 rear-facing overall with 1/5 labels and 1/5 vehicle-installation features** — joint-weakest in this comparison. Labels at 1/5 in both harness modes means the on-seat guidance is close to useless, which matters more on a seat bought for the character print than the spec sheet.

Disney Baby car seats are **rebadged Dorel Juvenile Group products** — the same shells, harnesses and hardware as the [Safety 1st](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) equivalents with licensed Disney fabric. They appear in NHTSA's database under make "DISNEY BABY" with the Safety 1st model names.

Every rated Dorel all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the Grow and Go, SlimRide EverSlim, Trimate and Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One.

Disney versions typically carry a **price premium of $20–70 over the Safety 1st original** for the fabric. You are paying for the licence, not for engineering.

## Conclusion

If you want this seat, buy the [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) at $127.99 instead — identical hardware, $22 cheaper, higher owner rating. The only thing the Disney version adds is the print.

More usefully: for $149.99, [Graco TriRide](graco-triride-3in1.md) scores **4/5 in all three modes with 5/5 instructions throughout** versus this platform's 2/5 rear-facing. That is a large measured upgrade at the same price, at the cost of a harness that is hard to tighten.

Whichever you buy on this platform, book a free CPST inspection — NHTSA's 1/5 install-features and 1/5 label scores mean the seat will not help you get it right.
