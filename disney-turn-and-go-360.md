## Safety

Expect the platform's position: "Meets federal Side Impact standard," **FMVSS 213a not named**, and "Baby Safety Alliance Verified" rather than a federal certification.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for any Turn and Go variant in either branding.

Disney Baby car seats are **rebadged Dorel Juvenile Group products** — the same shells, harnesses and hardware as the [Safety 1st](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) equivalents with licensed Disney fabric. They appear in NHTSA's database under make "DISNEY BABY" with the Safety 1st model names.

Every rated Dorel all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the Grow and Go, SlimRide EverSlim, Trimate and Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One.

Disney versions typically carry a **price premium of $20–70 over the Safety 1st original** for the fabric. You are paying for the licence, not for engineering.

A zero here means "not yet in the database," not "clean." The [Safety 1st Turn and Go 360](safety1st-turn-and-go-360.md) shares the platform and carries two complaints — **both describing the metal cable between seat and base failing:**

- "The metal cables on the car seat that attach it to base, one of them is fraying."
- "Metal tether connecting the seat to the ro[t]ating platform on the base **has broke. Allowing the seat to come completely off the base.**"

That second report is the same failure class that put the Evenflo Revolve360 line under open federal investigation **AQ24001**. One report is not six, and no investigation exists here — but it is the specific thing to inspect on this platform.

## Cons

- Platform rates **3.74★ across 188 ratings**; the DLX sibling has **66% would recommend**

- Both platform complaints describe the **metal seat-to-base cable fraying or breaking**, one allowing the seat to come off the base
- **1.0★ from its only review**
- **No FMVSS 213a claim**; "Baby Safety Alliance Verified" is not a federal standard
- Never rated by NHTSA; rated Dorel siblings score 2/5 rear-facing
- Heavy and bulky; poor fit for a small infant
- No published expiration date

## Conclusion

The worst value on this list. $399.99 is more than [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) ($359.99: three modes, 213a *and* 213b, zero complaints, and a rated sibling that scored **5/5 rear-facing** — the best rotating score in NHTSA's dataset) and $120 more than [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) ($279.99: 1 complaint, 4.75★, 85% would recommend, best install subscore of any rotating seat).

You would be paying a $70 licensing premium over a platform that owners rate 3.74★, whose swivel lever they cannot reach, and whose two federal complaints are about the cable holding the seat to its base.

If you want this platform, buy the [Safety 1st original](safety1st-turn-and-go-360.md) at $329.99 and inspect the seat-to-base cables. If you want a rotating seat, buy the [Graco](graco-easyturn-360.md) or the [Joie](joie-matcha-spin-si.md).
