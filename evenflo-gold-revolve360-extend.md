## Safety

**Covered by open NHTSA investigation AQ24001**, opened January 5, 2024 and still open. NHTSA: the Revolve360 "appears not to comply with a number of FMVSS No. 213 requirements, including but not limited to the means of installation in S5.3.2 and labeling requirements in S5.5," and the agency is "aware of at least six crash incidents in the field during which the Revolve360's shell... reportedly separated from its detachable base." The investigation explicitly extends to "other similar Revolve360 products, which also use detachable bases." NHTSA notes "Evenflo has continued to certify and offer the Revolve360 products for sale." Full detail on the [Revolve360 Slim page](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md).

The **Gold** trim adds SensorSafe, a sensor-equipped chest clip containing a **button battery**. On the [Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) a parent filed: "the internal components of the chest clip **including the button battery** were in my child's hand and she could have easily swallowed it." Button-battery ingestion causes rapid oesophageal burns. That is a consideration specific to the Gold line.

Side impact: L.I.F.E. Guard — marketing terminology. **No FMVSS 213a citation.**

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for this model. Evenflo's rated seats, for brand calibration:

**Recall 25C003000** (2025): child seats and travel systems shipped **missing the USA recall registration card**, FMVSS 213 non-compliance, 1,068 units. Evenflo mailed cards free. Minor in itself — but a seat you cannot register is a seat you will not be told about when the next recall lands, which on this platform is a live concern.

Complaint themes: harness/buckle 14 · install/LATCH 7 · crash 5 · fabric/foam 5 · breakage 2 · rotation 2. Trend: 16 (2024) · 3 (2025) · 2 (2026).

Representative filings:
- "the 360-degree rotating car seat **failed to lock securely into position while the child** [was in it]" — filed on a January 2025 build in a 2017 GMC Acadia
- "I was in a car accident. **The revolve 360 seat detached from the base.**" — the exact failure AQ24001 is investigating
- "The padding (foam) underneath the cover of the car seat is cracked/broken... EvenFlo is claiming for it to be just as safe" — the same foam failure that got the [Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) recalled (25C010000, 324,997 units)

Evenflo brand-wide: **3,541 complaints, 270 injuries, 41 recalls, 25 investigations** across 321 model records — the worst of any brand in this comparison on every normalisation. Eight recalls since 2023 covering over 547,000 units, most for FMVSS 213 non-compliance. Full table on the [Revolve360 Slim page](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md#brand-track-record--evenflo).

Two patterns worth carrying: Evenflo's **All4One scored 4/5 rear-facing from NHTSA and was later recalled** (25C011000) for a rear-facing recline mechanism that shifts out of position — so a good ease-of-use score does not predict compliance. And Evenflo's remedies lag peers: mailed tape for a 325,000-unit foam recall, versus [Graco](graco-turn2me.md) replacing seats outright for 26C003000.


## Reliability & owner feedback

4.77★ from 120 ratings — the highest average of any Evenflo here. Target flags that many Evenflo five-star reviews are cross-posted from evenflo.com/influenster and collected as part of a promotion, so read it down.

For contrast, the non-Gold [Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md) sits at 4.47★ with **55% would recommend** — the lowest recommend rate in this comparison — on 564 ratings. Same seat, larger and less promotional sample.

Owner complaints across the Extend line: crotch buckle flops flat so the baby sits on it, rattling while driving, harness hard to tighten or loosening while driving, seat/base wobble with the indicator not going fully green (one owner's CPST advised pausing use), rotation needing force, and cover removal taking fifteen manual pages.

## Conclusion

Do not buy. This is the most expensive seat in the comparison, it sits on the platform under active federal investigation for FMVSS 213 non-compliance, and its own complaint record contains both of the failures that investigation is about: a seat that detached from its base in a crash, and a rotation mechanism that failed to lock with a child in it.

The Gold trim adds SensorSafe for roughly $180 over the [standard Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md). A child-left-behind alarm is a real safety feature — but not one worth buying a seat whose shell-to-base attachment is under investigation, and the sensor clip introduces a button battery that a sibling-model filing says a child got hold of.

At $529.99 you could buy [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) and [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) together, both with clean records, and still have change. If you want one premium rotating seat, [Joie Pepper Spin SI](joie-pepper-spin-si.md) at $449.99 has four modes to 120 lb, 213a and 213b compliance, and zero complaints.
