
# Evenflo Revolve 360 Extend All-in-One Rotating Convertible Car Seat

**Price:** $349.99 (reg. $351.99) · **Target TCIN** 92391010 · [Back to summary](README.md)
**Target product page:** [https://www.target.com/p/evenflo-revolve-360-extend-all-in-one-rotating-convertible-car-seat-revere-gray/-/A-92391010](https://www.target.com/p/evenflo-revolve-360-extend-all-in-one-rotating-convertible-car-seat-revere-gray/-/A-92391010)

> ## ⚠️ Do not buy — covered by an active federal compliance investigation
>
> This seat is flagged in NHTSA's database under **investigation AQ24001**, opened January 5, 2024 by the Office of Vehicle Safety Compliance and still open as of September 2026. NHTSA's own words: the Revolve360 "appears not to comply with a number of FMVSS No. 213 requirements, including but not limited to the means of installation in S5.3.2 and labeling requirements in S5.5." The agency is "aware of at least six crash incidents in the field during which the Revolve360's shell... reportedly separated from its detachable base," and states that **"Other similar Revolve360 products, which also use detachable bases, appear to pose the same issues with the FMVSS No. 213 requirements."** The Extend uses a detachable base. NHTSA notes that "Evenflo has continued to certify and offer the Revolve360 products for sale."
>
> The Extend itself has **no recall** as of September 2026. Its sibling models do: **26C004000** (Reo by Revolve360 — headrest may shift position during a crash, fails FMVSS 213, 59,661 units, remedy still "under development"), **25C010000** ([Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) — headrest foam choking hazard, 324,997 units), **26C005000** (Revolve180 Litemax NXT — miscalibrated recline indicator, fails FMVSS 213, 35,951 units).
>
> If you already own one: register it with Evenflo at **1-800-233-5921** so you receive notice if a remedy is ordered, and check nhtsa.gov/recalls against your manufacture date periodically.

The most capable Evenflo here — three modes to 120 lb, 50 lb rear-facing, better install scores than the [Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md). Also the lowest "would recommend" rate of any seat in this comparison at 55%, on a platform under active federal investigation.

## Safety

- **Covered by open investigation AQ24001** — see the warning box. NHTSA's database carries the investigation flag against the Revolve360 Extend record directly (`investigationsCount: 1`), not merely by inference from the "similar products" language.
- Side impact: Evenflo cites **"L.I.F.E. Guard — Linear Impact Force Engineered to improve safety performance in side impact crashes."** This is a marketing designation, not a standard citation. Unlike [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) and [Britax Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md), neither Revolve360 seat cites **FMVSS 213a** by name.
- JPMA Certified; meets FMVSS 213; Evenflo claims structural integrity, rollover, and temperature testing.
- **No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating exists** for any Revolve360 model. Evenflo's rated seats, for brand calibration:
  - **Evenflo All4One**: RF 4/5, FF 4/5, **booster 3/5** — securing-child 1/5 and vehicle-install-features 1/5 in booster. *This model was subsequently recalled (25C011000) for a rear-facing recline mechanism that may shift out of position.*
  - **Evenflo EveryFit**: RF 4/5, FF 5/5, **booster 3/5** — securing-child 1/5 in booster.
  - Pattern across Evenflo's rated seats: solid in harness modes, consistently weak in booster mode. Relevant here, since booster is this seat's third mode.

## Reliability

- 4.47★ from 564 ratings, but **only 55% would recommend — the lowest of any seat in this comparison.**
- Subscores: installation 4.28, comfort 4.18, durability 4.17, quality 3.90, value 3.89.
- Distribution 74% five-star / **6% one-star**.
- Target flags that some five-star entries are cross-posted from evenflo.com/influenster and "collected as part of a promotion." Read the star average down.

Top owner complaints:
- **Crotch buckle flops flat so the baby sits on it:** "I have to fish it out from underneath him."
- **Rattling noise while driving.**
- **Harness hard to tighten, or it "loosen[s] up while driving."**
- **Seat/base wobble once attached, plus the indicator not going fully green.** One owner's CPST advised pausing use; Evenflo support felt "condescending."
- **Rotation sometimes requires force:** "you have to really slam the seat."
- Documentation gaps, especially cover removal: "it takes FIFTEEN pages" and the cover comes off in five or six pieces.
- Snug lengthwise; value and quality doubts at this price.

The 55% recommend rate against a 4.47 star average is the widest gap in this dataset. That pattern — high stars, low recommend — usually means owners like the concept and regret the purchase.

## Ease of use

**Works well (per CSFTL and owners):**
- Open belt path; premium LATCH connectors; QR-code manual.
- One install can cover both harness modes.
- On-the-go recline — better than [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) or [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md), which lock recline at install.
- Broad fit range: a 4 lb preemie doll fit with the folded insert; a 7 lb newborn with it unfolded (not without it). Forward-facing worked for a 45 lb four-year-old and a 57 lb / 47.5" seven-year-old.
- Harness and buckle both stow on the seat for booster conversion.
- Generous padding; roomy for taller and heavier babies.
- Free live video install support from a certified tech via ParentLink.

**Friction points:**
- **LockStrong is not a lock-off** — the belt must still be locked separately. Owners who assume otherwise get a loose install.
- Full 360° rotation and forward-facing lockup require the most-reclined rear-facing setting.
- The rotated position does not lock; CSFTL testers found it "somehow magically stayed put," which is not a specification.
- **Booster mode is weak:** CSFTL's 10-year-old (65 lb, 52.5") fit but sat at the top setting and would outgrow it quickly. The 120 lb ceiling is nominal. The booster was also hard for a seven-year-old to buckle alone.
- 31" depth rear-facing fully reclined — pushes front seats forward.
- Cover removal takes 15 manual pages and five to six pieces.
- Never use shell or base separately.
- CSFTL: heavy and unwieldy; cupholder holes felt "quite sharp" when removed; children reported thin back padding and narrow shoulder room.

## Pros

- Three modes to 120 lb — most capable Evenflo here.
- Rear-facing to **50 lb / 48"** — ties the [Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) for best rear-facing capacity in the comparison.
- Installation subscore 4.28 — much better than the Slim's 3.48.
- On-the-go recline.
- Rotates in both directions.
- Excellent newborn fit via the folding insert; three crotch-buckle positions with no weight rules.
- Generous padding; wide fit range for larger babies.

## Cons

- **Covered by open NHTSA compliance investigation AQ24001; shell-base separation in ≥6 reported crashes.**
- Three sibling recalls on the same platform in two years, one of which (Reo, 26C004000) is a crash-performance failure with **no remedy developed yet**.
- **55% would recommend — lowest in the comparison.**
- Owners report the same foam-shedding failure that got the [Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) recalled, on this un-recalled model.
- Rotation-lock indicator failures (half red / half green, refuses to lock).
- Harness loosens while driving; strap fraying reported.
- Crotch buckle flops flat under the child.
- 19.8" wide and 31" deep reclined — bulky.
- Booster mode's 120 lb ceiling is nominal; real-world runway is short.
- 90-day warranty.
- No baseless use at all.
- Cover removal is a 15-page, six-piece job.
- Side-impact claim is marketing language, not a 213a citation.

## Brand track record — Evenflo

From the full NHTSA dataset (321 Evenflo model records, 54 active):

- **3,541 total complaints, 270 reported injuries, 41 distinct recalls, 25 distinct investigations** — worst of the five brands in this comparison by a large margin.

**Recalls since 2023 — eight campaigns, over 547,000 units:**

| Campaign | Date | Subject | Units |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23C005000 | 2023 | Missing Warning Labels / FMVSS 213 | 1 |
| 25C003000 | 2025 | Missing Recall Registration Card / FMVSS 213 | 1,068 |
| 25C008000 | 2025 | Missing Instruction Manual (Titan 65) / FMVSS 213 | 36,694 |
| 25C010000 | 2025 | Choking Hazard from Foam in Headrest ([Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md)) | 324,997 |
| 25C011000 | 2025 | Recline Position May Shift (All4One) / FMVSS 213 | 74,710 |
| 26C002000 | 2026 | Incorrect Information in Spanish Manual / FMVSS 213 | 14,020 |
| **26C004000** | **2026** | **Headrest May Shift in a Crash (Reo by Revolve360) / FMVSS 213** | **59,661** |
| 26C005000 | 2026 | Incorrect Recline Indicator (Revolve180 Litemax NXT) / FMVSS 213 | 35,951 |

Key points:
1. **Most of these are FMVSS 213 non-compliance** — the seats did not meet the federal standard as sold, rather than wearing out in service.
2. **The Revolve platform has been recalled three times across variants in two years.** Reo (headrest shifts in a crash), Revolve180 Litemax NXT (recline indicator), Revolve360 Slim (foam). The Extend is the platform sibling that has not yet been named.
3. **Evenflo's All4One — which NHTSA had rated 4/5 rear-facing — was later recalled** for a rear-facing recline mechanism that may shift out of position. Good ease-of-use scores do not predict compliance.
4. **Remedy quality lags peers.** The 325,000-unit foam recall was remedied with mailed tape seal. The Reo crash-performance recall had interim letters sent September 1, 2026 with the actual remedy still "under development." Compare [Graco](graco-turn2me.md)'s 26C003000, where Graco replaced the seat outright.

## Conclusion

**Do not buy.** On paper this is the second-most-capable seat in the comparison — three modes to 120 lb, 50 lb rear-facing, respectable install scores, on-the-go recline. The problem is not the design brief; it is the evidence around its execution.

You would be paying $349.99 for a seat whose platform is under open federal investigation for FMVSS 213 non-compliance, whose siblings have been recalled three times in two years including once for headrest displacement in a crash with no remedy yet developed, whose owners report the exact foam failure that got the [Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) recalled, and which only 55% of buyers would recommend to someone else.

For the same money or less, with three modes and a clean record: [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) at $359.99 (3 modes, 0 complaints, best NHTSA-rated sibling here) or [Graco 4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md) at $295.99 (4 modes to 120 lb, real NHTSA ease-of-use data, no rotation). For rotation on a budget: [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) at $279.99.
