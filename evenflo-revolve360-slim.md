
# Evenflo Revolve 360 Slim 2-in-1 Rotational Convertible Car Seat

**Price:** $291.99 (reg. $364.99) · **Target TCIN** 92391009 · [Back to summary](README.md)

> ## ⚠️ Do not buy — active federal compliance investigation and an open recall
>
> This seat is covered by **NHTSA investigation AQ24001**, opened January 5, 2024 by the Office of Vehicle Safety Compliance, still open as of September 2026. NHTSA's own words: the Revolve360 "appears not to comply with a number of FMVSS No. 213 requirements, including but not limited to the means of installation in S5.3.2 and labeling requirements in S5.5." NHTSA is "aware of at least six crash incidents in the field during which the Revolve360's shell... reportedly separated from its detachable base." The agency notes that "Evenflo has continued to certify and offer the Revolve360 products for sale."
>
> This seat is also subject to **recall 25C010000** (reported September 25, 2025): Revolve360 Slim and Gold Revolve360 Slim manufactured between December 1, 2022 and December 8, 2024 — **324,997 units** — because "the headrest foam is not adequately secured away from the child, which can allow the child to pick off pieces of foam," a choking hazard. Evenflo's remedy was to mail owners additional tape seal and installation instructions.
>
> If you already own this seat: check the manufacture date sticker against the recall range, contact Evenflo customer service at **1-800-233-5921**, and look up your model at nhtsa.gov/recalls. Do not discard the seat without a replacement plan — an unremedied seat in the recall range is still safer than no seat.

## Verdict

Lowest-priced rotating seat with the best rear-facing weight limit (50 lb) and the narrowest footprint (16.75"). None of that outweighs an open federal compliance investigation involving shell-base separation in six reported crashes, plus a 325,000-unit recall, plus the worst installation subscore in the comparison.

## Safety

- **Open investigation AQ24001** — see the warning box above. Opened 2024-01-05, `dateClosed: null`. NHTSA's stated purpose: "Further examination of Evenflo's certification of the Revolve360 products as compliant with FMVSS No. 213 is necessary." Six supporting documents on file including a test report titled "TEST REPORT: EVENFLO GOLD REVOLVE360 CONVERTIBLE CHILD SEAT."
- **Recall 25C010000** — headrest foam choking hazard, 324,997 units, remedy = mailed tape seal.
- The investigation explicitly extends beyond the base Revolve360: "Other similar Revolve360 products, which also use detachable bases, appear to pose the same issues with the FMVSS No. 213 requirements." NHTSA's database flags this investigation against both the Revolve360 Slim and the [Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md).
- Side-impact protection: no specification stated on the Slim's listing. The [Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md) cites "L.I.F.E. Guard — Linear Impact Force Engineered," which is marketing terminology rather than a standard citation. Neither seat cites **FMVSS 213a** the way [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) and [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) do.
- JPMA Certified; Evenflo claims structural integrity, rollover and temperature testing.
- **No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating exists** for any Revolve360 model. Evenflo seats that *are* rated, for brand calibration:
  - **Evenflo All4One**: RF 4/5, FF 4/5, **booster 3/5** — with securing-child 1/5 and vehicle-install-features 1/5 in booster. *This same model was later recalled — see brand section.*
  - **Evenflo EveryFit**: RF 4/5, FF 5/5, booster 3/5
  - Evenflo's rated seats score respectably in harness modes and badly in booster mode.

## Reliability

- 4.34★ from 584 ratings; **68% would recommend**.
- Subscores: quality 3.95, durability 3.79, comfort 3.69, value 3.62, **installation 3.48 — lowest single subscore of any seat in this comparison.**
- Distribution 70% five-star / **8% one-star**.
- Top owner complaints:
  - **Crotch/hip buckle placement** is the most-repeated issue — straps ride down toward the thighs and the buckle "digs into his legs," with reports of marks or bruises.
  - **Wobble in the rotating seat** — "the rotational part is wobbly as can be." Evenflo's stated position is that this movement is by design.
  - **Harness straps** finicky, thin, prone to twisting — matching the NHTSA harness-loosening complaints.
  - "A nightmare to install and even worse to adjust" — consistent with the 3.48 install subscore.
  - Rattling noise when empty.
  - Multiple frustrations with returns and refunds from both Evenflo and Target.

## Ease of use

**Works well (per CSFTL and owners):**
- Open belt path; premium LATCH connectors; QR-code manual.
- One install can cover both modes, so harness-mode owners may only install "one time. Ever."
- The foldable newborn insert is genuinely clever — a 4 lb preemie doll fit using the folded insert; a 7 lb newborn fit with it unfolded (but not without it).
- On-the-go recline without reinstalling — better than [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) or [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md), both of which lock recline at install.
- Slim fit in smaller vehicles is a repeated owner win: "The slim model fits perfectly in our vehicles."
- Machine-washable, dryer-safe on low.

**Friction points:**
- **LockStrong is not a lock-off.** You must still lock the seat belt separately. Owners who assume otherwise get a loose install.
- With LATCH, over-tightening prevents the LockStrong arm from closing.
- Full 360° rotation and forward-facing lockup require the most-reclined rear-facing setting — an unintuitive interaction.
- The rotated position does not lock; testers found it "somehow magically stayed put," which is not a specification.
- CSFTL: heavy and unwieldy; cupholder holes felt "quite sharp" when removed; children reported thin back padding and narrow shoulder room.
- Watch for belt-guide stress marks if the belt slips while closing LockStrong.
- Never use shell or base separately.

## Pros

- Narrowest seat in the comparison at 16.75".
- Best rear-facing limits here: 50 lb / 48" — ties [Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md) and beats [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) (5–50 lb) on the low end.
- On-the-go recline.
- Excellent newborn fit via the folding insert.
- Standard (non-magnetic) chest clip.
- Lowest price of the rotating seats after [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md).
- Free live video install support from a certified tech.

## Cons

- **Open NHTSA federal compliance investigation (AQ24001), shell-base separation in ≥6 reported crashes.**
- **Recall 25C010000 — 324,997 units, choking hazard.**
- 28 complaints, 2 reported injuries.
- Worst installation subscore in the comparison (3.48).
- LockStrong is not a true lock-off, and the documentation invites misuse.
- Heaviest seat here at 33.6 lb.
- Cannot be used without the base at all — no travel or taxi use.
- **90-day warranty** — every other seat here gives a year.
- No booster mode despite a 6-year age cap.
- Crotch buckle geometry causes reported bruising.
- Seat-on-base wobble that the manufacturer declines to treat as a defect.
- Forward-facing limits documented inconsistently.

## Brand track record — Evenflo

From the full NHTSA dataset (321 Evenflo model records, 54 active):

- **3,541 total complaints, 270 reported injuries, 41 distinct recalls, 25 distinct investigations** — by far the worst of the five brands here.

**Recalls since 2023 alone — eight campaigns, over 547,000 units:**

| Campaign | Date | Subject | Units |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23C005000 | 2023 | Missing Warning Labels / FMVSS 213 | 1 |
| 25C003000 | 2025 | Missing Recall Registration Card / FMVSS 213 | 1,068 |
| 25C008000 | 2025 | Missing Instruction Manual (Titan 65) / FMVSS 213 | 36,694 |
| **25C010000** | **2025** | **Choking Hazard from Foam in Headrest (Revolve360 Slim — this seat)** | **324,997** |
| 25C011000 | 2025 | Recline Position May Shift (All4One) / FMVSS 213 | 74,710 |
| 26C002000 | 2026 | Incorrect Information in Spanish Manual / FMVSS 213 | 14,020 |
| 26C004000 | 2026 | Headrest May Shift in a Crash (Reo by Revolve360) / FMVSS 213 | 59,661 |
| 26C005000 | 2026 | Incorrect Recline Indicator (Revolve180 Litemax NXT) / FMVSS 213 | 35,951 |

Three observations that matter more than the raw count:

1. **Multiple recalls are FMVSS 213 non-compliance, not wear-out.** The seats did not meet the standard as sold.
2. **The Revolve platform is recalled repeatedly across variants.** Reo by Revolve360 (headrest shifts in a crash, remedy still "under development" as of July 2026), Revolve180 Litemax NXT (miscalibrated recline indicator), Revolve360 Slim (foam). That is three distinct compliance failures on one product family.
3. **Evenflo's All4One — a seat NHTSA had rated 4/5 rear-facing — was later recalled** for a rear-facing recline mechanism that "may shift out of proper position," 74,710 units, replacement seats issued. A good ease-of-use score does not predict compliance.

Evenflo's remedy quality is also weaker than peers'. For the 325,000-unit foam recall the fix was mailed tape. Compare [Graco](graco-turn2me.md)'s 26C003000, where Graco replaced the seat outright.

## Conclusion

**Do not buy.** The narrowest footprint and the 50 lb rear-facing limit are real advantages, and $291.99 is a real saving — but you would be buying into an open federal investigation into whether the seat complies with the child-restraint standard at all, on a platform whose sibling models have been recalled three times in two years for crash-performance failures, from the brand with 41 recalls and 25 investigations on record.

Better options at or near the price: [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) at $279.99 (cheaper, clean record, rotates both ways) or [Graco 4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md) at $295.99 (four modes to 120 lb, actual NHTSA ease-of-use data, no rotation).

If you need the 50 lb rear-facing limit specifically, [Britax Galaxy 360 Slim](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) offers 5–50 lb rear-facing with a clean record, ClickTight installation, an anti-rebound panel, and explicit FMVSS 213a certification — at $52 more.
