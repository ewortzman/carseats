## NHTSA record

| Item | Count |
|---|---|
| Complaints | **102** — highest of any seat in this comparison |
| Injuries | **1** |
| Recalls | **1 — 14C003000** |
| Open investigations | 0 |

**Recall 14C003000** — Evenflo recalled convertible and harnessed restraints including "Symphony (including 65, LX, 65 E3, and DLX)" because the **buckle may be difficult to unlatch**. **1,368,649 units** — the largest recall touching any seat in this comparison by two orders of magnitude. Evenflo notified owners in April 2014 and supplied replacement buckles. Historical and long remedied, but it establishes that the buckle on this platform has failed at scale before.

Complaint themes: **harness/buckle 81** · install/LATCH 37 · fabric/foam 20 · crash 13 · breakage 11 · recline 5.

Representative filings — note two are from a Child Passenger Safety Technician:
- "Seat purchased in September. Mom went to move seat and found harness strap frayed."
- "Harness strap is fraying. Siblings seat also doing and was report previously today. Less fraying on this one than siblings. **Ann Moenart CPST**"
- "this carseat has been used for 2 of my children, never been in any accidents and when I took the cover off to wash it the headpiece foam is released and broken."

**Harness webbing fraying is the signature failure of this platform** — 81 of 102 filings touch the harness or buckle. Frayed webbing loses tensile strength, which is the one property a harness exists to provide. Two of the reports come from a certified technician, not a worried parent.

Evenflo brand-wide: **3,541 complaints, 270 injuries, 41 recalls, 25 investigations** across 321 model records — the worst of any brand in this comparison on every normalisation. Eight recalls since 2023 covering over 547,000 units, most for FMVSS 213 non-compliance. Full table on the [Revolve360 Slim page](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md#brand-track-record--evenflo).

Two patterns worth carrying: Evenflo's **All4One scored 4/5 rear-facing from NHTSA and was later recalled** (25C011000) for a rear-facing recline mechanism that shifts out of position — so a good ease-of-use score does not predict compliance. And Evenflo's remedies lag peers: mailed tape for a 325,000-unit foam recall, versus [Graco](graco-turn2me.md) replacing seats outright for 26C003000.

## Conclusion

Do not buy. The refreshed name and the 4.70★ from 49 reviews are not evidence; the 102 complaints with 81 harness or buckle failures are. Frayed harness webbing is not a cosmetic defect — it is the load path that restrains your child, and a CPST filed two of those reports.

At this price there is no reason to take the risk. [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) is $40 cheaper with steel lock-offs, an explicit FMVSS 213a claim, 16.7" width and zero complaints. [Graco Extend2Fit 3-in-1](graco-extend2fit-3in1.md) is $21 cheaper with rear-facing to 50 lb. [Graco 4Ever DLX](graco-4ever-dlx.md) is $32 more with a verified 4/5/4 NHTSA rating and four modes.

If you already own a Symphony of any generation: **pull the cover and inspect the harness webbing at the hip slots and shoulder slots for fraying.** Stop using the seat and contact Evenflo at 1-800-233-5921 if you find any.
