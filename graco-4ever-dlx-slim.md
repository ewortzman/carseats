## Safety

**This is the only seat in the comparison with a genuine NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating on its own platform.**

**Graco 4Ever DLX** (all-in-one, mfg. Feb 2021) — NHTSA official ratings:

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install features | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **4/5** | 4 | 5 | 3 | 5 |
| Forward-facing | **5/5** | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Booster | **4/5** | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 |

Notable: **5/5 on instructions in all three modes** and 4–5 on labels in all three. Compare the pattern for the rotating seats' nearest rated proxies — [Joie](joie-matcha-spin-si.md)'s Chili Spin 360 scored 1/5 on forward-facing labels, [Chicco](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md)'s Fit4 scored 1/5 forward-facing and booster, and [Evenflo](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md)'s All4One scored 1/5 forward-facing. Documentation quality is where this seat separates itself, and documentation is what determines whether a caregiver installs correctly.

Caveat: the rating is for the 4Ever DLX, not the 4Ever DLX **Slim**. The Slim is a narrower variant of the same platform with the same SnugLock install, same Simply Safe Adjust harness, same four modes. It is the closest proxy relationship in this entire comparison — much closer than the sibling comparisons used for the rotating seats.

Other safety points:
- Meets and, per Graco, exceeds FMVSS 213 frontal crash criteria. Graco cites "ProtectPlus Engineered" testing for side and frontal impact plus EPS energy-absorbing foam and a steel-reinforced frame, and ASTM compliance.
- **FMVSS 213a is not explicitly cited** on the Target listing for this model — unlike [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) and [Britax Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md). Check the manufacture date sticker; units made after June 30, 2025 must comply.
- **No rotating mechanism = no rotation-lock failure mode.** Every rotating seat in this comparison has complaints about jamming, sticking, half-locked indicators, or wobble. This seat structurally cannot have those problems. That is a real, if unglamorous, safety advantage.
- Highest lower-anchor limit here at 45 lb, so you can stay on LATCH longer before switching to a belt install.
- Backless booster cannot use LATCH or tether, so **buckle it when empty** — otherwise it is a projectile.

## Reliability

- 4.76★ from 85 ratings; **100% would recommend** — the only seat in the comparison at 100%.
- Distribution: 81% five-star, 15% four-star, 2% three-star, 0% two-star, 1% one-star. **Almost no negative tail** — contrast the 8% one-star tails on [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) and [Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md), and 15% on [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md).
- Caveat: only 85 reviews on this specific Slim/Thompson listing, and Target does not publish site-wide subscores for it. The two detailed reviews gave 5/5 on comfort, durability, installation, and quality, with cleaning and value at 4–5.
- The underlying 4Ever DLX platform has years of field history and a CSFTL recommendation.

**CSFTL verdict: recommended.** "Another solid option in Graco's ever-growing lineup," easy to use and fitting many sizes. This is the only seat in the comparison with an unqualified CSFTL recommendation — CSFTL declined to recommend [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md), pulled [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) from its list, flagged the LockStrong issue on the [Revolve360](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md), and has not reviewed [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md), [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md), or [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) at all.

## Ease of use

**Works well:**
- **RapidRemove cover** — comes off in 60 seconds without uninstalling the seat, and it is multi-piece so you wash only the soiled section. Best cleaning system in the comparison. Contrast [Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md), where cover removal takes 15 manual pages and five to six pieces, or the non-zip [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md), where an owner rated ease of cleaning 1/5.
- Dedicated forward-facing belt lock-off, which you may bypass if you prefer to lock the belt yourself.
- Onboard harness storage compartment plus separate LATCH storage bars for each belt path.
- Lightest seat here at 22.75 lb, and 9.6 lb in backless mode — genuinely portable.
- Simple backless conversion.
- FAA approved (harnessed).
- Long usable range: 4 lb to 120 lb, ten years.
- Rear-facing bubble recline indicator; pool noodle or rolled towel explicitly permitted.
- Owner praise centres on installation ease ("They fit nicely 3 across"), the washable fabric, comfort on long trips, all-in-one longevity, and arriving assembled.

**Friction points (CSFTL and owners):**
- **Preemie and newborn fit requires the inserts.** CSFTL: "We would not recommend this seat for preemies without the inserts in use." The 7 lb doll fit with inserts, barely acceptable without.
- More cover pieces to reassemble after washing.
- Manual instructions for lower-anchor storage were unclear; Graco agreed to revisit.
- **Cupholders are permanent once clicked on** — and owners report they trap crumbs, with one describing having to disassemble the shell after vomit lodged in the grooves.
- The back fabric panel connects upward, so one mess can mean washing nearly everything.
- **No forward-facing recline indicator** — you rely on weight-based position rules.
- CSFTL found the under-40-lb forward-facing recline tilted the headrest into the vehicle headrest in a 2016 Kia Sedona, limiting headrest height for taller children. Vehicle-dependent; test in yours.
- Backless mode sits high, making self-buckling tricky.
- Harness reported "more difficult to adjust than the full size 4Ever DLX."
- One owner reported a toddler sitting too upright rear-facing.
- Replacement pads and inserts reportedly unavailable.

**Fit (CSFTL, full-size 4Ever DLX):** 6-year-old (47", 51 lb) fit well harnessed and in highback booster; 8-year-old (51") needed the top headrest setting with roughly a year left; 10-year-old (52", 67 lb) got a good belt fit backless.

## Cons

- **Does not rotate.** If you chose this category for the rotation, this seat does not deliver it. That is the whole trade.
- **21.5" wide** — widest seat in the comparison. "Slim" refers to a 10% reduction at the cupholders versus the standard 4Ever DLX, not to overall width. Poor for three-across; [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) and [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) are 17".
- Cupholders permanent and crumb-trapping.
- No forward-facing recline indicator.
- Newborn and preemie fit depends on the inserts.
- Multi-piece cover is more work to reassemble.
- Forward-facing recline under 40 lb tilted the headrest into the vehicle headrest in at least one minivan.
- Replacement pads/inserts reportedly hard to get.
- Harness adjustment reported harder than on the full-size 4Ever DLX.
- FMVSS 213a not explicitly cited on the listing.
- Rear-facing tops out at 40 lb — lower than [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) or the Evenflo seats (50 lb). Rear-facing longer is the highest-leverage safety choice, so this is a genuine limitation.
- Forward-facing limits documented inconsistently on the listing (26.5–65 lb vs. 22–120 lb).

## Brand track record — Graco

From the full NHTSA dataset (343 Graco model records, 40 active):

- **1,356 total complaints, 103 reported injuries, 16 distinct recalls, 3 distinct investigations** across all history. Very high sales volume, so absolute counts overstate the per-unit rate.
- Most recent recall: **26C003000** (May 2026) — SnugRide Turn & Slide infant seats and Modes Nest travel systems, "infant seat carrier may detach from the base in a crash," fails FMVSS 213, 5,126 units. **Graco replaced the seats free of charge** and advised belt installation without the base in the interim — a materially stronger remedy than [Evenflo](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md)'s mailed-tape fix for 325,000 units.
- NHTSA rates 20 Graco seats — the largest rated sample of any brand here. Graco consistently scores 4–5 on instructions and labels, which is the brand's genuine strength and directly relevant to a seat you will reconfigure four times over ten years.
- Graco sits comfortably between [Chicco](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md)/[Joie](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) (small, clean catalogues) and [Evenflo](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) (41 recalls, 25 investigations) on compliance.
- Graco Consumer Care visibly replies to negative reviews with a support number.

## Conclusion

Strictly on evidence, this is the best-supported purchase on the list: a real NHTSA ease-of-use rating with 5/5 instructions in all modes, the only unqualified CSFTL recommendation, 100% of owners recommending it, a mature high-volume platform with a near-zero complaint record, four modes to 120 lb, ten years of life, and the lowest cost of ownership at $29.60/yr.

It also does not rotate, and you were shopping rotating seats. Be honest about which you want:

- **You want rotation because bending into a car seat hurts your back or you have a C-section recovery, a bad knee, or a tall vehicle.** Then buy a rotating seat — [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) at $279.99, or [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) at $359.99 if you want booster mode. Rotation is the feature, and this seat does not have it.
- **You want rotation because it looked convenient.** Then buy this instead. You get better documentation, better longevity, a verified federal ease-of-use score, a lighter seat, a better cleaning system, and you save money — at the cost of leaning in to buckle.

The one real safety trade against the rotating options: 40 lb rear-facing here versus 50 lb on [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) and the Evenflo seats. Rear-facing longer matters. If your child is large and you want to rear-face past 40 lb, [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) is the seat that combines a 50 lb rear-facing limit with a clean safety record — at a worse ownership experience and $48 more.

Also worth noting: at 21.5" wide this is the widest seat here. If you need three-across, it is out, regardless of everything above.
