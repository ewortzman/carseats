## Safety

**Cites FMVSS 213a explicitly** — "Side-impact tested according to FMVSS 213a with the built-in 5-point harness system," plus Graco ProtectPlus Engineered and FMVSS 213. Only a handful of seats in this comparison name 213a: this one, [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md), [Britax Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md), [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md), [Chicco OneFit Max](chicco-onefit-max.md), [Chicco Fit360 Zip](chicco-fit360-zip.md), [Britax One4Life](britax-one4life.md), and the Joie SI line.

**Official NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** (mfg. Feb 2021) — one of only ~14 seats in this comparison with a real rating:

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **4/5** | 4 | 5 | 3 | **5** |
| Forward-facing | **5/5** | 5 | 5 | 4 | **5** |
| Booster | **4/5** | 3 | 4 | 4 | **5** |

The sibling **4Ever with Safety Surround** variant scores slightly worse (RF vehicle-install 1/5), so buy the plain DLX rather than the Safety Surround version if you have the choice.

No rotating mechanism means none of the jam / half-lock / wobble failure modes that every rotating seat in this comparison reports.

## Pros

- Real NHTSA rating: 4/5 RF, **5/5 FF**, 4/5 booster, 5/5 instructions in every mode
- **FMVSS 213a cited explicitly**
- 6,173 ratings / 89% recommend — the most credible review base in the comparison
- CSFTL recommended
- 4 modes, 4–120 lb, 10-year life
- 23 lb — among the lightest here
- Integrated belt lock-off
- Highest lower-anchor limit (45 lb)
- RapidRemove cover off in 60 seconds
- $40 cheaper than the [Slim variant](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md)
- FAA certified harnessed

## Cons

- **Does not rotate**
- **21.5" wide** — too wide for three-across
- Rear-facing caps at 40 lb; [Britax Poplar](britax-poplar.md), [Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md), [UPPAbaby Rove](uppababy-rove.md) and the Evenflo rotating seats reach 50 lb
- Scratchy fabric; pad velcro wears out and replacements aren't sold
- Short LATCH straps and crotch buckle
- No forward-facing recline indicator (weight-based rules instead)
- Avoid the "4Ever with Safety Surround" variant — worse NHTSA install score

## Conclusion

If you do not need rotation, **buy this instead of the [4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md)** — it is $40 cheaper, it is the variant NHTSA actually rated, and it carries 6,173 ratings instead of 85. The Slim's only advantage is being narrower at the cupholders, and even then it measures 21.5" overall, the same as this seat.

Against the rotating seats: you give up rotation and a 50 lb rear-facing limit, and you gain a verified federal ease-of-use score, four modes to 120 lb, ten years of life, and the best-evidenced reliability record on the list. At $255.99 that is $25.60/yr — the cheapest cost of ownership of any seat in this comparison.

Rule out only if you need three-across (21.5" is too wide — use [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) or [Graco SlimFit3 LX](graco-slimfit3-lx.md) at 16.7") or you want rotation.
