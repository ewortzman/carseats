# Graco EasyTurn 360 Rotating Convertible Car Seat

**Price:** $279.99 (reg. $349.99) · **Target TCIN** 92695220 · [Back to summary](README.md)

## Verdict

**Top pick of the eight.** Cheapest seat in the group with the cleanest safety record, rotation in both directions, and a 17" footprint. The only real knock is that it is the newest model here, so its field history is thin — and it has no booster mode, so it is not a "last seat."

## Safety

- Meets FMVSS 213. Graco cites "ProtectPlus Engineered" testing for side and frontal impact plus EPP energy-absorbing foam and a steel-reinforced frame.
- **No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating exists** — this seat is not in NHTSA's 126-seat rated dataset. See [Sources & methodology](sources.md).
- FMVSS 213a (the newer side-impact standard) is not explicitly cited on the product page the way [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) and [Britax Galaxy 360](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) cite it. Given the model's recency, units now shipping are almost certainly 213a-era, but check the manufacture date sticker.

## Reliability

- 4.75★ from 579 ratings; **85% would recommend** — second-highest in the group.
- Target flags it "rarely returned" and "5k+ bought in last month."
- Subscores: installation 4.17, comfort 4.59, durability 4.48, quality 4.34, value 4.41. No subscore below 4.0 — the only seat in this comparison that can say that.
- 84% five-star.

## Ease of use

**Works well:**
- Rotates in both directions with one hand; audible click confirms lock.
- SnugLock install advertised under a minute; push-button InRight LATCH with audible click.
- Easy-read level indicator.
- LATCH stows in an onboard compartment.
- Standard (non-magnetic) chest clip — avoids the failure mode dominating [Chicco Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) complaints.

**Friction points (from owner reviews):**
- One owner found the turn handle stiffer than the [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md).
- "Base is very tall" — won't sit flush when reclined.
- Lower-anchor straps dangle.
- One owner reported a rear-facing design issue where a child's feet can reach a button affecting latching. Worth noting: this is the same class of complaint CSFTL raised about the [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) lock-off panel.
- Recline cannot be changed after installation.

## Pros

- Lowest price in the comparison.
- Rotates both directions — [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) at a higher price only rotates rear-facing.
- 17" width; workable for three-across or a narrow back seat.
- Cleanest safety record among the rotating seats.
- Highest ease-of-installation subscore of any rotating seat here.
- Non-magnetic chest clip.

## Cons

- No booster mode — you will buy another seat around 65 lb. Contrast [4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md) (to 120 lb) or [Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md) (to 120 lb).
- Not FAA certified — rules out air travel use.
- Newest model here; least field history, so the clean record is less informative than it looks.
- Recline locked in at install.
- Rear-facing height limit is documented inconsistently between Target's spec table and Graco's own answers.

## Brand track record — Graco

From the full NHTSA child-seat dataset (343 Graco model records, 40 currently active):

- **1,356 total complaints, 103 reported injuries, 16 distinct recalls, 3 distinct investigations** across the brand's history.
- Most recent recall: **26C003000** (May 2026) — SnugRide Turn & Slide infant seats and Modes Nest travel systems, "infant seat carrier may detach from the base in a crash," fails FMVSS 213, 5,126 units. Graco replaced seats free of charge. **Does not affect this seat**, but it is a detach-from-base failure on a different Graco rotating/click-in product, which is worth knowing.
- Graco sits between [Chicco](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) (small, clean) and [Evenflo](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) (large, poor) on compliance. Volume is very high, so absolute counts overstate the per-unit rate.
- Graco responds to negative reviews and issues replacement remedies rather than tape-and-instruction fixes — a better pattern than Evenflo's.

## Conclusion

If you want a rotating seat and do not need booster mode or air travel, this is the one to buy. It costs $40–80 less than every other rotating seat in the comparison, rotates both directions (which [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) does not), has a materially better install score than [Revolve360 Slim](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) or [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md), and carries none of the compliance baggage of the Evenflo line or the chest-clip complaint pattern of the [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md).

The honest caveat: its record is clean partly because it is new. If you want a clean record backed by years of field data instead, that is [4Ever DLX Slim](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md) — but it does not rotate.

Register the seat with Graco on purchase. Recall notification depends on it.
