## Safety

Certifications listed are **FMVSS 213 only — no FMVSS 213a claim.** Graco's Q&A reply dodged a direct 213a confirmation, citing "internal standards" for seats made before the deadline. Since 213a is mandatory for units built after June 30, 2025, check the manufacture date sticker; recent stock should comply even though the listing doesn't say so.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for the SlimFit3 LX. Its predecessor **Graco SlimFit** is rated (mfg. June 2024) and the numbers are a warning on install features:

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | 4/5 | 5 | 3 | **1/5** | 5 |
| Forward-facing | 4/5 | 5 | 3 | **2/5** | 3 |
| Booster | 4/5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 |

A 1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features is among the worst scores in NHTSA's entire rated dataset. The LX adds a forward-facing lock-off, which may improve on that, but it is unrated and rear-facing still has no lock-off.

Also approved with inflatable seat belts — but Graco says do not use the shoulder-belt lock-off with those.

Praise: fast install, genuinely fits three across (confirmed in a Hyundai Tucson), works in compact cars and narrow third rows, washable cover, light enough to move between vehicles.

Complaints:
- **Head slumping forward when asleep** — the dominant negative, raised repeatedly, with owners voicing positional-asphyxia worry; one called it "terrible and dangerous." This is a recline-geometry issue and the seat only has 4 recline positions.
- Cramped for taller rear-facing toddlers (bent legs).
- Narrow for larger babies.
- Some difficulty adjusting straps.

Target flags that many five-star reviews are promotion-collected and syndicated from gracobaby.com.

## Cons

- **No FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing
- Predecessor SlimFit scored **1/5** on rear-facing vehicle-installation features from NHTSA

- Cramped for tall toddlers and wide babies
- Does not rotate

- Five-star reviews are promotion-inflated

## Conclusion

The best answer in this comparison to "I need three car seats across" or "I have a small car" — 16.7" and 19.2 lb, with the best install subscore among the narrow seats. [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) matches the width at $183.99, adds a rear-facing lock-off and an explicit FMVSS 213a claim, and is the better buy on paper; this Graco counters with a much larger review base (1,363 vs. 127) and a higher install score.

The head-slump complaints are the reason this isn't a top-three pick overall. If your child is a car sleeper, test the recline before committing, and use a rolled towel or pool noodle under the base if the manual permits it.
