## Safety

**Tested to FMVSS 213a and 213b** — the Joie SI line is the only one in this comparison claiming both.

**Joie Chili Spin 360** — the one rated Joie rotating seat, and the best rear-facing score of any rotating seat in NHTSA's entire 126-seat dataset (mfg. Aug 2024):

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **5/5** | 5 | 3 | **5** | **5** |
| Forward-facing | **4/5** | 4 | **1/5** | **5** | 4 |

Only the Cybex Sirona S and Nuna Revv ($500–600) matched that rear-facing 5/5. **Vehicle-installation features score 5/5 in both modes** — the axis most seats here fail. The weak spot is forward-facing labels at 1/5.

This is the strongest single piece of safety evidence for any rotating seat in this comparison. A 5/5 rear-facing overall with 5/5 on both vehicle-installation features and instructions is what you want from a seat you will install once and use for years. Only the labels (3/5 rear-facing, **1/5 forward-facing**) let it down — and label quality is Joie's brand-wide weak axis, the same as [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md).

The rated record is for the **Chili Spin 360**, the same platform under its prior name.

## Reliability & owner feedback

**4.08★ from 97 ratings — the second-lowest average in this comparison**, behind only [Britax One4Life Slim](britax-one4life-slim.md) (3.95). Target does not publish attribute subscores on this listing.

That is a striking gap: the best NHTSA ease-of-use score of any rotating seat, paired with a mediocre owner rating. The most likely explanation, based on the [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) review pattern on the same platform family, is the **stiff non-adjustable thigh and side bolsters** that must stay installed until 40 lb — owners of the Matcha report cramped fit, a crotch strap sitting mid-thigh, and comfort scoring 3.63. NHTSA does not measure child comfort.

Note also that this is a **2-in-1** at $339.99 while the [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) is a 3-in-1 at $359.99 — $20 more for a third mode.

## Conclusion

Genuinely the best-*measured* rotating seat here. If you weight federal ease-of-use data above owner sentiment, this is the buy: 5/5 rear-facing overall, 5/5 install features, 5/5 instructions, 213a and 213b, SIP pods, one benign complaint.

Two reasons it is not the top recommendation. First, **4.08★ across 97 owners** is real information that NHTSA's protocol does not capture — almost certainly the rigid bolsters, which are the dominant complaint on the platform. Second, for $20 more the [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) gives you a third mode and a booster to 100 lb on the same platform with the same standards claim.

**Buy the [Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) instead unless you specifically want the 2-in-1.** Either way, sit your child in it before committing — the bolsters are the thing people regret.
