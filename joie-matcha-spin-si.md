# Joie Matcha Spin SI Rotating 3-in-1 Convertible Car Seat

**Price:** $359.99 (reg. $399.99) · **Target TCIN** 94808520 · [Back to summary](README.md)

## Verdict

**Cleanest brand record in the comparison and the best NHTSA-rated sibling seat of any product here — but the most expensive, with a dominant comfort complaint about stiff, non-adjustable thigh bolsters.** Strong runner-up to [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) if you want three modes and 360° rotation in both directions.

## Safety

- Joie states the seat "meets the newest safety standards for both frontal and side impact protection." Note: Target's certification field reads "No Certifications," which is a listing-data gap rather than a finding — but it means side-impact compliance is not independently confirmable from the listing. Ask Joie directly whether this unit is FMVSS 213a-certified, or check the manufacture date sticker.
- **No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating exists** for the Matcha Spin SI. **But its closest sibling is rated, and it scored the best of any rotating seat in NHTSA's entire 126-seat dataset:**

  **Joie Chili Spin 360** (rotating convertible, mfg. Aug 2024):

  | Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install features | Instructions |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Rear-facing | **5/5** | 5 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
  | Forward-facing | **4/5** | 4 | 1 | 5 | 4 |

  Only the Cybex Sirona S and Nuna Revv (both $500–600) matched that rear-facing 5/5 among rotating seats. Weak spot in both Joie and the broader rotating category: **labels** (3/5 RF, 1/5 FF).

  This is a sibling, not the same seat — but it is the strongest available proxy in this whole comparison, and it points the same direction as the complaint data.

## Reliability

- 4.58★ from 63 ratings; **68% would recommend**.
- Subscores: value 4.56, quality 4.47, ease of cleaning 4.45, installation 4.25, **comfort 3.63** (weakest).
- Distribution 76% five-star / 14% four / 2% three / 6% two / 2% one.
- Target flags "2k+ bought in last month" and **"Rarely returned"** — one of only two seats here with that badge (the other is [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md)).
- Small review base. Treat the numbers as directional.

## Ease of use

**Works well:**
- Smooth, effortless rotation for transfers — the dominant praise theme.
- 360° in *every* harnessed mode, plus 180° in booster so an older child self-loads. Broadest rotation coverage of any seat here — [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md) only rotates rear-facing, and [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) has no booster mode to rotate in.
- 15-position no-rethread harness, headrest and harness move together on one button.
- Install rated easy and intuitive by most (4.25 subscore).
- Premium feel for the money: "feels like a luxury car seat for a budget price point!"
- Reported to calm babies transitioning out of an infant carrier.
- Cup holders removable, dishwasher top-rack safe.

**Friction points:**
- **Recline cannot be adjusted after installation** — you must get it right the first time. Same limitation as [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md); worse than [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md), which has dual-mode recline indicators, or the Evenflo seats, which offer on-the-go recline.
- Covers are hard to remove and reinstall.
- Bulky in sedans.
- A minority report difficulty getting a tight install.
- LATCH specifics are not documented on the listing — you will need the manual.

## Pros

- Best NHTSA-rated sibling of any seat in this comparison (Chili Spin 360: 5/5 RF overall).
- Zero complaints, zero recalls, zero investigations — both for this seat and brand-wide.
- 360° rotation in all harness modes plus 180° in booster — broadest rotation coverage here.
- 15-position no-rethread harness.
- Three modes to 100 lb.
- 18.8" width is mid-pack, and it demonstrably fits a Corolla behind a 5'4" driver.
- "Rarely returned" badge.
- Highest value subscore (4.56) despite being the priciest — owners feel they got their money's worth.

## Cons

- **Highest price in the comparison at $359.99** — $80 more than [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md).
- **Stiff thigh bolsters are the dominant complaint.** They are "quite stiff, not pliable, moveable, or otherwise adjustable" and must stay installed until 40 lb. One parent's 25 lb / 11-month-old was already a tight fit. The crotch strap sits mid-thigh or near the knees on some children. Target's own review summary flags "concerns... about the snug fit due to the side bolsters." Comfort subscore 3.63 reflects this.
- Recline locked at install.
- Covers hard to refit after washing.
- Forward-facing limits documented inconsistently (30–100 lb / 40–57" vs. 30–65 lb / up to 49").
- Seat expiration not published on the listing — you must find it on the seat label.
- Only 63 reviews; 68% would recommend is mediocre.
- No CSFTL teardown exists for this seat, so no independent expert install assessment. (CSFTL has reviewed no Matcha model — a real evidence gap compared with [Fit360](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md), [Turn2Me](graco-turn2me.md), [Revolve360](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md), and [4Ever DLX](graco-4ever-dlx-slim.md), all of which have full teardowns.)
- Base permanently attached — no travel/baseless use.

## Brand track record — Joie

From the full NHTSA dataset (8 Joie model records, **all 8 active**):

- **10 total complaints, 0 reported injuries, 0 recalls, 0 investigations** — across the brand's entire US history.
- Zero on every adverse metric. The best brand record in this comparison by a wide margin.
- Compare: [Chicco](chicco-fit360-cleartex.md) 178 complaints / 2 recalls · [Britax](britax-galaxy-360-slim.md) 972 / 22 recalls / 6 investigations · [Graco](graco-turn2me.md) 1,356 / 16 recalls / 3 investigations · [Evenflo](evenflo-revolve360-slim.md) 3,541 / 41 recalls / 25 investigations.
- Important qualifier: Joie has by far the smallest US catalogue and the shortest US history of the five brands. A clean record on 8 models is not the same evidence as a clean record on 343. The brand is unproven at scale rather than proven safe at scale.
- NHTSA rates two Joie seats (Chili Spin 360, Basil 2-in-1, Mint Latch, Rue), and the Chili Spin 360's 5/5 rear-facing overall is the brand's strongest signal.

## Conclusion

The best *evidence* of any rotating seat here — clean brand, clean model, and a sibling with the top NHTSA rear-facing ease-of-use score in the entire rated dataset. If the thigh bolsters fit your child and you want booster mode plus rotation in every direction, this is the buy.

Two reasons it is the runner-up rather than the pick: it costs $80 more than [EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md), and the bolster complaint is not a minor gripe — it is the single most repeated negative, it is not adjustable, and you cannot remove the bolsters until 40 lb. If your child is chunky or long-legged, sit them in it in-store before committing.

Secondary consideration: no CSFTL teardown exists, so nobody independent has verified the install quality the way they have for the Chicco, Graco, and Evenflo seats. The favourable evidence here is complaint-absence plus a sibling rating — both real, both indirect.

Register the seat with Joie on purchase.
