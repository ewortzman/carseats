## Safety

**Tested to FMVSS 213a and 213b** — Joie states the SI line is "tested to the newest safety standards, including FMVSS 213a and 213b." This is a manufacturer claim; no third-party data appears on the listing.

The **TurnLock indicator** (green locked / red unlocked) directly addresses the failure mode that dominates rotating-seat complaints elsewhere: [Evenflo Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-revolve360-extend.md) owners report indicators showing "half red/half green" and refusing to lock, and the [Gold Extend](evenflo-gold-revolve360-extend.md) has a filing where the seat "failed to lock securely into position while the child" was in it. An unambiguous two-state indicator on both sides is the right design answer.

**LockTight works with either LATCH or belt** — more flexible than most lock-offs here, which are belt-only or forward-facing-only.

**Joie Chili Spin 360** — the one rated Joie rotating seat, and the best rear-facing score of any rotating seat in NHTSA's entire 126-seat dataset (mfg. Aug 2024):

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **5/5** | 5 | 3 | **5** | **5** |
| Forward-facing | **4/5** | 4 | **1/5** | **5** | 4 |

Only the Cybex Sirona S and Nuna Revv ($500–600) matched that rear-facing 5/5. **Vehicle-installation features score 5/5 in both modes** — the axis most seats here fail. The weak spot is forward-facing labels at 1/5.

Note: unlike the Evenflo rotating seats, this seat's base is not a separate detachable shell-and-base system of the kind under investigation in **AQ24001**.

## Reliability & owner feedback

4.76★ from 49 ratings; 92% five-star, 4% three-star, 4% one-star. "100% would recommend" rests on 6 recommendations — thin.

Subscores (small sample): comfort 5.0, durability 5.0, installation 5.0, quality 5.0, ease of cleaning 4.8.

Praise: rotation eases loading — "Makes getting baby in and out so easy and fit perfectly in my Nissan Altima"; sturdy and "easy to install"; comfort; booster conversion.

Complaints: one 1★ review reports "The backless booster part comes loose and then will not reinstall into the base," plus trouble levelling. A 5★ reviewer calls it "a beast," too bulky for most travel bags.

Caveat: 49 ratings is not enough to characterise reliability. The platform's rated sibling scores well with NHTSA, and the brand record is spotless, but this specific seat is lightly evidenced.

## Conclusion

**If you want a premium rotating seat, buy this rather than the [Evenflo Gold Revolve360 Extend](evenflo-gold-revolve360-extend.md).** It is $80 cheaper, has four modes instead of three, claims both 213a and 213b, has a proper two-state rotation-lock indicator, and carries zero complaints on the cleanest brand record here — against a seat under open federal investigation whose own filings include a shell detaching in a crash.

Against the value picks: it costs $170 more than [Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) and $90 more than [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md). What the money buys is the fourth mode (booster to 120 lb, so no further seat purchase), 180° rotation in booster so an older child self-loads, and the 15-position harness.

Two practical warnings: at 32.4 lb it is the heaviest seat here, so it is not moving between cars often; and with 49 ratings, one owner already reports the backless-booster component failing to reseat. Keep the receipt and exercise the booster conversion early.
