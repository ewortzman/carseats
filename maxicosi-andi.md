## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** documented in this research pass — confirm on the listing and check the manufacture date sticker.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for the Andi. Maxi-Cosi's rated seats are [Romi](maxicosi-romi.md) (3/5 both modes) and [Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md) (3/5 all three modes, 1/5 rear-facing install features). The Andi is a higher-tier product than either, so those numbers are a floor rather than a prediction — but Dorel has no rated seat above 3/5 rear-facing in this comparison.

Maxi-Cosi is a **Dorel Juvenile Group** brand, as are Safety 1st and the Disney Baby car seat line. Every rated Dorel convertible or all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**: [Maxi-Cosi Romi](maxicosi-romi.md) 3/5, [Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md) 3/5, [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) 2/5, [EverSlim](safety1st-everslim.md) 2/5, [Trimate](safety1st-trimate.md) 2/5. Four of the five score **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features**.

Dorel recall history in this comparison: **21C003000** (Maxi-Cosi Pria 85, 119,863 units, potential head injury with lap-belt use, **"No remedy has been established at this time"**) and **21C004000** (Pria 70, 4,000 units, same defect — remedied with replacement labels plus a free booster seat).

## Conclusion

The only Maxi-Cosi in this comparison I would not warn you off, and the ratings back that: 4.70★ on 185 owners with a clean federal record is a real signal, and the Pria recalls do not touch it.

The problem is price. At $499.99 for a two-mode seat you are paying more than [Joie Pepper Spin SI](joie-pepper-spin-si.md) ($449.99, four modes, rotates, 213a and 213b, zero complaints) and nearly double [Joie Saffron SI](joie-saffron-si.md) ($249.99, four modes to 120 lb, 213a and 213b, SIP pods, zero complaints, 88% recommend).

Buy it if you want premium materials and the brand's best-reviewed seat, and you are comfortable buying a second seat for the booster years. Otherwise the Joies deliver more for less. Before ordering, confirm the per-mode limits and whether your unit is FMVSS 213a-certified — neither was verifiable from the data gathered here.
