## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** documented — confirm on the listing and check the manufacture date.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for the Kani. Maxi-Cosi's rated seats: [Romi](maxicosi-romi.md) 3/5 both modes, [Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md) 3/5 all modes with 1/5 rear-facing install features.

Maxi-Cosi is a **Dorel Juvenile Group** brand, as are Safety 1st and the Disney Baby car seat line. Every rated Dorel convertible or all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**: [Maxi-Cosi Romi](maxicosi-romi.md) 3/5, [Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md) 3/5, [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) 2/5, [EverSlim](safety1st-everslim.md) 2/5, [Trimate](safety1st-trimate.md) 2/5. Four of the five score **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features**.

Dorel recall history in this comparison: **21C003000** (Maxi-Cosi Pria 85, 119,863 units, potential head injury with lap-belt use, **"No remedy has been established at this time"**) and **21C004000** (Pria 70, 4,000 units, same defect — remedied with replacement labels plus a free booster seat).

## Cons

- **Only 28 ratings** — almost no owner evidence
- **$379.99 for a 2-mode seat**; no booster
- **Never rated by NHTSA**; no Dorel seat here rates above 3/5 rear-facing
- **No FMVSS 213a claim** documented

- Specs not verified in this pass
- Does not rotate

## Conclusion

Awkwardly placed. It is $120 cheaper than the [Andi](maxicosi-andi.md) but has a seventh of the reviews, and $130 more than [Joie Saffron SI](joie-saffron-si.md), which offers four modes to 120 lb, FMVSS 213a *and* 213b, SIP pods, zero complaints and 88% would recommend.

At $379.99, [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) ($359.99) rotates, has three modes, and its rated sibling scored the best rear-facing ease-of-use mark in NHTSA's dataset.

No reason to choose the Kani unless you specifically want this seat's fit or fabric and have checked it in your car. If you want a premium Maxi-Cosi, the [Andi](maxicosi-andi.md) has the evidence.
