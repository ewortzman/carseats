**Recall 21C003000** — Maxi-Cosi **Pria 85** convertible seats. "When the seat is installed in the forward facing position and secured to the car using the lap belt without also using the top tether, the seat may not adequately protect the child from head injury." FMVSS 213 non-compliance. **119,863 units.** NHTSA's record: **"No remedy has been established at this time."** Owner letters mailed October 8, 2021. Dorel customer service: 1-877-657-9546.

**Recall 21C004000** — Maxi-Cosi **Pria 70**, same defect, 4,000 units. Remedied with replacement labels and instruction booklets **plus a free Maxi-Cosi Rodi Sport booster** for children 40–100 lb.

These cover the Pria 70 and 85, not the current Pria Pure Cosi. But an unremedied 119,863-unit FMVSS 213 head-injury recall on the platform name is the single most serious unresolved recall touching any brand in this comparison.

Complaint themes across the family: harness/buckle 7 · install/LATCH 4 · headrest 4 · fabric/foam 2 · breakage 1.

## Conclusion

Skip it. The current seat is not the recalled Pria 70 or 85, but you are buying into a platform whose most serious recall — a forward-facing head-injury non-compliance affecting nearly 120,000 seats — is recorded by NHTSA as having no established remedy. Add 1/5 rear-facing install features, 11 complaints including frayed harness webbing, and 4.06★ across 514 ratings.

If you want a flame-retardant-free all-in-one at this price, [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) is $86 cheaper with GREENGUARD Gold fabric, steel lock-offs, an explicit 213a claim, 16.7" width and zero complaints. [Joie Saffron SI](joie-saffron-si.md) is $20 cheaper with four modes to 120 lb, 213a *and* 213b, SIP pods and zero complaints.

**If you own any Maxi-Cosi Pria: always use the top tether forward-facing.** That is the exact condition under which the recalled variants failed.
