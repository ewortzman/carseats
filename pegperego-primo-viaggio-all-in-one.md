Three complaints is a small number in absolute terms. What matters is what they describe. Themes: harness/buckle 3 · breakage 1 · install 1.

- "The contact owns a Peg Perego Primo Viaggio convertible car seat... **the chest clip broke in half in an attempt to secure the child in its car seat.**"
- "PEG PEREGO INFANT CAR SEAT **ASSEMBLED INCORRECTLY FROM MANUFACTURER.** SEE ATTACHED IMAGES. METAL BUCKLE CAME THROUGH PADDING. LOOKING BEHIND PADDING, **BELT WAS THREADED THROUGH ONLY ONE LOOP. CHILD HAD BEEN INSECURELY FASTENED FROM [DAY] 1**"
- "MY HUSBAND LOOSENED THE STRAPS OF MY DAUGHTERS CARSEAT, PLACED HER IN THE SEAT THEN BUCKLED IT AND RETIGHTENED THE STRAPS TO BE SNUG ENOUGH SO SHE COULDN'T MOVE MUCH. THEN AS WE ARE DRIVING WE CAME TO A SUDDEN STOP AN[D]..."

The second is the serious one. A harness routed through a single loop instead of both is a **manufacturing quality-control failure that defeats the restraint** — and the parent had been using the seat that way since purchase without knowing. The first, a chest clip breaking in half during normal buckling, is also a load-path component failing.

All three complaints touch the harness or buckle. With only three filings you cannot call it a rate, but there is no offsetting evidence here either — no rating, no teardown, one review.

## Conclusion

Do not buy. This is the least-evidenced seat on the list, and the little evidence that exists is bad: every federal complaint concerns the harness or buckle, one describes the chest clip snapping in half during normal use, and one describes the factory routing the harness webbing through a single loop — a defect the parent only discovered after the metal buckle pushed through the padding.

There is no NHTSA rating, no expert teardown, one customer review, and no visible price.

Buy something documented. [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) $183.99, [Graco 4Ever DLX](graco-4ever-dlx.md) $255.99, [Joie Saffron SI](joie-saffron-si.md) $249.99 and [Britax Poplar](britax-poplar.md) $307.99 all have federal ratings or large review bases behind them.

**If you already own one: pull the cover and verify the harness webbing is routed through both loops on the splitter plate**, and inspect the chest clip. That is the specific failure two owners reported.
