## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for the Ellaris. Its Dorel siblings that are rated all score **2/5 rear-facing overall** with **1/5 rear-facing vehicle-installation features** — [Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md), [EverSlim](safety1st-everslim.md), [Trimate](safety1st-trimate.md), and [Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md). There is no reason to expect the entry-level Ellaris to score better than the range above it.

Safety 1st and Maxi-Cosi are both **Dorel Juvenile Group** brands, as is the Disney Baby car seat line. Dorel's rated seats are the weakest group in NHTSA's dataset among the brands in this comparison: every Safety 1st and Maxi-Cosi all-in-one rated scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the Grow and Go, SlimRide EverSlim, Trimate and Pria All-in-One.

Dorel recall history relevant here: **21C003000** (Maxi-Cosi Pria 85, 119,863 units, potential head injury with lap-belt use, **"No remedy has been established at this time"**) and **21C004000** (Pria 70, 4,000 units, same defect, remedied with replacement labels plus a free booster seat). See [Maxi-Cosi Pria Pure Cosi](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md).

## Pros

- **$99.99** — lowest price of any convertible on Target
- Zero NHTSA complaints, recalls, investigations
- Meets FMVSS 213
- Lightweight and simple — a reasonable occasional-use or spare seat

## Cons

- **3.48★ from 101 ratings** — near the bottom of this comparison
- **Never rated by NHTSA**; every rated Dorel sibling scores 2/5 rear-facing
- No FMVSS 213a claim
- Fewest modes and shortest usable life of the Safety 1st range here
- No published expiration date
- Does not rotate

## Conclusion

Buy only if $99.99 is a hard ceiling. A correctly installed inexpensive seat protects a child properly, and the complaint record is clean — this is not a dangerous product.

But for $28 more, [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md) adds booster mode to 100 lb and has a 2,930-rating base at 4.13★; for $50 more, [Graco TriRide](graco-triride-3in1.md) adds a real NHTSA rating of 4/5 across all three modes. Both are better uses of the next $30–50 than almost anything else on this list.

If you do buy it: book a free CPST inspection. On an unrated seat with a 3.48★ average, that check is the only real quality control you have.
