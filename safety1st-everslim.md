## Safety

Meets FMVSS 213. **No FMVSS 213a claim** on the listing.

**Official NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** (Safety 1st SlimRide EverSlim, mfg. March 2024):

| Mode | Overall | Securing child | Labels | Vehicle install | Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear-facing | **2/5** | 3 | **2/5** | **1/5** | 3 |
| Forward-facing | **3/5** | 3 | **2/5** | 3 | 4 |
| Booster | **3/5** | **1/5** | 3 | **5** | 5 |

**2/5 rear-facing overall, 1/5 vehicle-installation features rear-facing, 2/5 labels in both harness modes, and 1/5 for securing the child in booster mode.** The one bright spot is 5/5 install features in booster, where the seat is just a belt guide.

Safety 1st and Maxi-Cosi are both **Dorel Juvenile Group** brands, as is the Disney Baby car seat line. Dorel's rated seats are the weakest group in NHTSA's dataset among the brands in this comparison: every Safety 1st and Maxi-Cosi all-in-one rated scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the Grow and Go, SlimRide EverSlim, Trimate and Pria All-in-One.

Dorel recall history relevant here: **21C003000** (Maxi-Cosi Pria 85, 119,863 units, potential head injury with lap-belt use, **"No remedy has been established at this time"**) and **21C004000** (Pria 70, 4,000 units, same defect, remedied with replacement labels plus a free booster seat). See [Maxi-Cosi Pria Pure Cosi](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md).

## Reliability & owner feedback

4.20★ from 145 ratings. Target does not publish full attribute subscores on this listing.

Mid-pack for the category and below every Graco, Chicco and Joie convertible here.

**CSFTL: favourable — "a winner of a seat!"** They were "pleasantly surprised," found it "overall fairly easy to use" and well suited to tight spaces, and describe it as a well-featured, sensibly priced, narrow seat that installs "without too much fuss." Their measurements: **17" at the widest point** (Safety 1st markets 16.75"), making it "a solid option in the world of 3 seats across"; **22 lb** harnessed or as a highback booster, **6 lb** as a backless booster. They note it is not especially long-lasting by child height but consider that acceptable given the four modes.

That verdict is worth weighing against NHTSA's 2/5 rear-facing rating — the two disagree, and they are measuring different things. NHTSA scored the install *features* and labelling; CSFTL actually installed it.

- $183.99

## Cons

- **NHTSA rates it 2/5 rear-facing overall** with **1/5 rear-facing vehicle-installation features**
- **1/5 for securing the child in booster mode**
- Labels 2/5 in both harness modes
- No FMVSS 213a claim
- 4.20★ — below every Graco, Chicco and Joie convertible here
- Rear-facing caps at 40 lb / 40" — the 40" height limit is short
- No published expiration date
- Does not rotate

## Conclusion

Priced directly against [Chicco Fit3x](chicco-fit3x.md) at the same $183.99 — and the Chicco wins clearly: 16.7" documented width, steel LockSure lock-offs in both directions, an explicit FMVSS 213a claim, dual bubble level indicators, GREENGUARD Gold flame-retardant-free fabric, 4.62★ with 87% would recommend, and no 1/5 scores anywhere.

The EverSlim's clean complaint record is real and worth something, but NHTSA measured this platform and found the rear-facing install genuinely hard to get right. Pay the same money for the Chicco.

Buy the EverSlim only if you find it materially discounted and you will have a CPST check the install.
