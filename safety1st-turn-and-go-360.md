## Safety

The listing states it "Meets federal Side Impact standard" and is NHTSA compliant. **FMVSS 213a is not named anywhere** — the side-impact reference is generic. Certification shown is "Baby Safety Alliance Verified," which is not a federal standard.

Genuinely good install hardware for the price: **SecureTech red-to-green tension indicators on the base**, a **built-in belt lock-off arm**, and a **visual top-tether indicator**. Three separate proper-use confirmations, which is more than most seats here provide.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for any Turn and Go variant.

Safety 1st is a **Dorel Juvenile Group** brand, as are Maxi-Cosi and the Disney Baby car seat line. Every rated Dorel all-in-one in this comparison scores **2/5 or 3/5 rear-facing overall**, with **1/5 on rear-facing vehicle-installation features** on the [Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md), [EverSlim](safety1st-everslim.md), [Trimate](safety1st-trimate.md) and [Maxi-Cosi Pria All-in-One](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md). No Turn and Go variant has been rated.

Dorel recall history relevant here: **21C003000** (Maxi-Cosi Pria 85, 119,863 units, potential head injury with lap-belt use, **"No remedy has been established at this time"**).

**Two NHTSA complaints on the Turn and Go 360 record, and both describe the same failure — the metal cable tethering the seat to the rotating base failing:**
- "The metal cables on the car seat that attach it to base, one of them is fraying. I noticed this today when I brought the car seat inside to remove the car seat cover to wash."
- "Metal tether connecting the seat to the roaring [rotating] platform on the base **has broke. Allowing the seat to come completely off the base.**"

Two complaints is a small number. But the second one describes the seat separating entirely from its base — the same failure class that put the Evenflo Revolve360 line under open federal investigation **AQ24001** ("at least six crash incidents in the field during which the Revolve360's shell... reportedly separated from its detachable base"). One report is not six, and no investigation has been opened here. It is the specific thing to watch on this platform.

**If you own one: inspect the metal cables between seat and base for fraying whenever you remove the cover.**


## Reliability & owner feedback

**3.74★ from 188 ratings — the lowest-rated rotating seat in this comparison.** Distribution and subscores are published on the [DLX variant](safety1st-turn-and-go-360-dlx.md): 5★ 44%, 4★ 31%, 3★ 9%, 2★ 8%, 1★ 8%, with 66% would recommend.

Across the Turn and Go line, the consistent themes are:

Praise: buckling is the standout — "it is hands-down the best buckling mechanism of them all," credited to the magnetic chest clip. Rotation eases loading. One owner called install "a breeze done in five minutes."

Complaints:
- **The rotation release dominates** — "The swivel lever is almost impossible to reach in the rear facing position"; another found it "not an easy button to squeeze."
- The chest clip "doesn't always release."
- A seat that "can lift/separate slightly from the base" — worth reading alongside the NHTSA cable complaints above.
- Inability to reach the green tension zone on the indicator.
- A child's foot catching on the rear-facing latch.
- "HEAVY and bulky"; poor fit for a 4-month-old.

Dorel replies publicly to several of these.

- Owners independently report the seat "can lift/separate slightly from the base"

- Chest clip sometimes will not release
- **No FMVSS 213a claim**; "Baby Safety Alliance Verified" is not a federal standard

- Rear-facing height caps at 40"
- No published expiration date

## Conclusion

Hard to recommend. The install hardware is better than the price suggests — three proper-use indicators and a real lock-off arm — and the magnetic clip genuinely delights some owners. But a rotating seat whose rotation release you cannot reach rear-facing has failed at its one job, and that is the dominant complaint across 188 ratings at 3.74★.

The seat-to-base cable reports are the part I would not ignore. Two complaints is thin evidence, but one of them is the seat coming off the base, and owners separately report the seat lifting from the base. Inspect those cables.

[Graco EasyTurn 360](graco-easyturn-360.md) is $50 cheaper with 1 complaint, 4.75★, 85% would recommend and the best install subscore of any rotating seat — you lose booster mode. [Joie Matcha Spin SI](joie-matcha-spin-si.md) is $30 more with three modes, 213a/213b, zero complaints and the best NHTSA-rated sibling here.
