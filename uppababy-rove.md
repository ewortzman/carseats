## Safety

**No FMVSS 213a claim** appears on the listing — the notable gap on an otherwise strong safety specification. Check the manufacture date sticker.

What it does have is the best set of **install-confirmation** features of any non-rotating seat in this comparison:
- **Spring-loaded lock-off doors** that hold the belt without relying on the vehicle's automatic locking retractor
- **SmartSecure red-to-green tension indicator** — owners specifically credit this: "the red-to-green indicators ensure that we have a perfect install every time"
- Bubble level indicators and an open belt path

That combination attacks the exact axis NHTSA scores lowest across this comparison. Vehicle-installation features score 1/5 or 2/5 on the [Graco SlimFit](graco-slimfit-3in1.md), [Safety 1st Grow and Go](safety1st-grow-and-go.md), [EverSlim](safety1st-everslim.md), [Trimate](safety1st-trimate.md), [Maxi-Cosi Pria](maxicosi-pria-pure-cosi.md), [Britax Poplar](britax-poplar.md) forward-facing and [Baby Trend Cover Me](babytrend-cover-me.md).

Also: **no anti-rebound bar and no load leg**. The Multi-Directional Tether is UPPAbaby's substitute for rebound control.

**No NHTSA Ease-of-Use rating** for the Rove. NHTSA rates **UPPAbaby Knox** (convertible) and **Mesa / Mesa V2** (infant) — none in this comparison.

The **14 lb minimum** rear-facing means this is not a newborn seat. You need an infant seat first.

## Reliability & owner feedback

4.64★ from 105 ratings; 67% five-star, 30% four-star, 3% three-star, **0% two- or one-star**; **100% would recommend**.

Zero one- and two-star reviews across 105 ratings is the cleanest distribution of any seat in this comparison. Subscores appear on one review only (all 5.0), so treat those as noise — the distribution is the signal.

Praise: install ease and the tension indicator — "the red-to-green indicators ensure that we have a perfect install every time." "Buttery smooth fabric," comfort, extended rear-facing to 50 lb, washability, and a taller seating position so the child can see out.

Complaints:
- **Heavier and bigger than expected for travel** — one flyer said it is "very heavy and big" and had to check it.
- Several wanted rotation: "My only wish is that it had a 360° swivel feature."
- Harness hard to cinch to pinch-test tightness.
- Wide base would not fit a small sedan's middle seat.
- Rear-facing tether routing sits within kicking reach.
- Cup holder placement; the zipper sits under the child.
- **Repeat twisting of the vehicle belt** — "This is our second Rove and both seats do the same thing."

Target flags that many 4–5 star reviews came via a promotion or product-testing panel.

**CSFTL: reviewed favourably.** Their measurements differ from Target's: **19" at the shoulders, 14" at the base, 25.7 lb**. Harness heights 8" with the insert / 10" without, up to 16.5"; crotch buckle at 2", 3" and 4"; 5 recline positions. They confirm the 10-year expiry with the DOM and expiration labels on the bottom of the base, and note the forward-facing lower-anchor limit of 45 lb is "not the preferred method for forward facing mode." Replace the seat after any crash.

## Conclusion

The best-executed non-rotating premium seat in this comparison. The SmartSecure tension indicator and spring-loaded lock-off doors address the single most common real-world failure — an install that is not tight enough — and the owner distribution backs it up: zero one- or two-star reviews across 105 ratings is unique on this list.

Two hard limits. The **14 lb minimum** means it cannot be your first seat, and at $499.99 for two modes you will also buy a booster later. So the real cost of the UPPAbaby path is an infant seat plus this plus a booster.

If that suits how you were going to buy anyway, it is an easy recommendation. If you want one seat to cover birth through booster, [Joie Saffron SI](joie-saffron-si.md) at $249.99 or [Graco 4Ever DLX](graco-4ever-dlx.md) at $255.99 do that for half the money — with worse install hardware and, in the Graco's case, a verified 4/5/4 federal rating the Rove does not have.

One thing to confirm before ordering: whether your unit is **FMVSS 213a**-certified. The listing does not say, and at this price it should.
